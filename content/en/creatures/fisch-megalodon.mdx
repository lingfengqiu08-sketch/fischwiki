Few catches in Roblox's Fisch carry the same bragging rights as the Fisch Megalodon. This Exotic-rarity shark weighs a minimum of 50,000 kg, only appears during Megalodon Hunts behind Ancient Isle, and fights back hard with a massive progress speed penalty and a literal "rampage" ability. Whether you're chasing your first catch or farming the Terror of the Depths title, this guide breaks down everything you need to know: where it spawns, which rods and baits work best, how to force hunts with totems, and the stats behind one of the game's most iconic fish.

## How to Trigger a Megalodon Hunt

The Megalodon can only be caught during a Megalodon Hunt, a server event announced with the message: "There has been a Megalodon spotted past Ancient Isle!" The fish spawns in an abundance behind Ancient Isle, so once the event starts, multiple players can hook one.

Here are your options for triggering the event:

- **Wait naturally.** Megalodon Hunts occur randomly, and the fish despawns after 12 minutes if nobody catches it.
- **Use the Megalodon Hunt Totem.** This forces a hunt to begin on demand.
- **Spam Sundial Totems.** These skip the current day cycle, giving the Megalodon a chance to spawn at the start of each cycle. Based on community reports, it takes around 70 Sundial Totems on average to force a spawn. If nothing happens after 60–70 totems, try rejoining the server — the event may be bugged.

| Totem | Where to Buy | Cost | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sundial Totem | Hidden cave near Merlin's house, Sunstone Island | 2,000 C$ | Fast-spawning regular Megalodon |
| Megalodon Hunt Totem | — | Varies | Forcing a hunt instantly |
| Eclipse Totem | Cave behind Ancient Isle (hidden by leaves) | 250,000 C$ | Phantom Megalodon conditions |

If you're hunting on a budget, Sundial Totems are far more cost-efficient — especially since the regular Megalodon can be caught during Phantom and Ancient Megalodon Hunts too.

## Best Rods for Catching the Megalodon

Because the Megalodon weighs at least 50,000 kg, your rod's weight limit matters. Rods rated below the fish's minimum weight can't reel it in without special enchantments. Ideally, you want a rod with high resilience, high control, or a Stab ability to counter its erratic movement.

| Rod | Why It Works | Weight Limit Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steady Rod | Cheapest option at just 7,000 C$; pair with resilience boosts | Must handle 50,000 kg+ |
| No-Life Rod | High stats, community favorite for hunts | Handles weight naturally |
| Trident Rod | Strong control for the minigame | Handles weight naturally |
| Cerulean Fang Rod | High resilience option | Handles weight naturally |
| Rod of the Depths | Needs Herculean enchant | 25,000–40,000 kg class |

If your rod's weight limit falls between 25,000 and 40,000 kg (like the Crystalized Rod or Firework Rod), you'll need the **Herculean** enchantment. Rods under 25,000 kg require **Invincible** — but players generally advise against these, since low-tier rods also lack the resilience and control needed for the fight itself.

## Best Baits and Enchantments

The right bait makes a huge difference. Community consensus and the Fisch wiki both point to two standouts:

- **Deep Coral** – Grants 50 resilience, slowing the fishing bar dramatically and making the Megalodon's wild movements far more manageable. This is the safest pick.
- **Shark Head** – The Megalodon actually prefers this bait, increasing your hookup rate. It only gives 10 resilience, so it's better for confident players who can handle faster bar movement.

For enchantments, here's how to build around your rod:

| Situation | Recommended Enchantment |
| --- | --- |
| Low lure speed rod | Hasty |
| Want more Megalodon hookups | Immortal |
| Low resilience rod | Mystical or Piercing |
| No Exalted Relic available | Resilient, Long, or Chaotic |

## How to Actually Land the Catch

Hooking the Megalodon is only half the battle. When you do, a shimmering sound plays and an Exotic exclamation mark appears above your head — then the real fight begins.

Key mechanics to know:

- **-69% locked progress speed** means reeling feels glacial. Stay patient and don't panic.
- **Rampage ability:** The Megalodon has a 12% chance per fish movement to go on a rampage, shredding 25% of your current reeling progress and shrinking your control bar by 15% for 2 seconds. The chance drops by 1.2% per attack until it bottoms out at 6%, so the fight gets slightly easier the longer you survive.
- **Catch cutscene:** Successfully reeling it in triggers a unique cutscene, shared with other Megalodon variants, Krakens, and Scyllas.

A few final pro tips: the regular Megalodon appears during every hunt type, including Phantom and Ancient Hunts, so you can multi-task. Catching three Megalodons earns you the **Terror of the Depths** title. And per community reports, there's roughly a 5% chance of receiving a Megalodon Egg as a drop.

## FAQ

### Where does the Fisch Megalodon spawn?

The Fisch Megalodon spawns behind Ancient Isle during Megalodon Hunt events. There are no time, weather, or season requirements — you just need an active hunt. All three variants (regular, Ancient, and Phantom) spawn at Ancient Isle.

### What is the Megalodon worth in Fisch?

The base average value is around 28,285 C$, though the final payout scales with the fish's weight (50,000–140,700 kg) and any mutations applied. Mutated Megalodons can be worth significantly more.

### What's the cheapest way to catch the Megalodon?

Use the Steady Rod (7,000 C$) with a resilience-boosting enchantment or Deep Coral bait to tame the erratic bar movement. Stock up on Sundial Totems (2,000 C$ each on Sunstone Island) to speed up spawns.

### Can you still catch the Phantom Megalodon?

Yes. Earlier bugs that prevented the catch have been resolved, but the Phantom variant requires a naturally occurring eclipse during a real-life weekend, making it far rarer than the standard Fisch Megalodon.
