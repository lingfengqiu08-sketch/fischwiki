## What Is the Crested Relic in Fisch?

If you've been grinding the waters of Skycrest, chances are you've heard players talking about the **Fisch Crested Relic**. This rare Relic-tier fish is one of the most sought-after catches in the game, and for good reason — it's the key to unlocking the powerful Crested enchantment at the Keepers Altar.

Unlike typical fish, the Crested Relic doesn't care about weather, time of day, season, or bait. It can appear from **any body of water** at a fixed 1 in 2,000 chance, making it one of the more predictable rare items to farm — as long as you understand how its drop mechanics actually work.

In this guide, we'll break down everything you need to know: where to find it, how the drop rate functions, what it's worth, and whether the Crested enchantment is worth using on your favorite rod.

## Crested Relic Stats at a Glance

Before diving into strategy, here's a quick summary of the Crested Relic's core stats pulled from the official Fisch Wiki:

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rarity | Relic |
| Location | Skycrest (any body of water) |
| Catch Chance | 1 in 2,000 (fixed) |

| Base Weight (Average Cap) | 50 kg |
| Base Sell Value | 12,000 C$ |
| XP Reward | 2,500 XP |
| Weather/Time/Season/Bait Preferences | None |

### Why the "No Luck" Detail Matters

Here's the catch (pun intended): most rare fish in Fisch become easier to hook when you stack luck boosts, bait quality, and server-wide luck events. The Crested Relic **ignores all of that**. Its 1/2,000 chance is hardcoded.

This has two big implications:

- **Don't waste premium bait** specifically hunting this relic — bait preferences don't apply.
- **Luck events won't speed up your farm.** Your only real lever is casts per hour.

That makes the Crested Relic a pure volume game, which changes how you should approach farming it.

## How to Get the Crested Relic

Obtaining the Crested Relic is refreshingly simple compared to some of Fisch's location-locked rares:

1. **Equip any rod** — rod quality doesn't affect the drop chance.
2. **Fish in any body of water** — no specific fishing spot required, though many players farm in Skycrest since it's the relic's home region.
3. **Reel in catches until it drops** — expect roughly 2,000 casts on average for a single relic.

### Farming Efficiency Tips (Player Experience)

Since the drop rate is fixed, community reports consistently point to one strategy: **maximize catches per minute**. Here's what veteran players recommend:

| Tip | Why It Works |
| --- | --- |
| Use a fast-reel rod | More casts per hour = more 1/2,000 rolls |
| Fish in safe, open water | Avoids obstacles that can break your line and waste casts |
| Play during AFK-friendly sessions | The grind is pure volume, so passive farming adds up |
| Don't chase luck boosts for this relic | They simply don't apply — save them for other rares |
| Watch for the catch quips | Lines like "I feel its power.." signal a relic pull |

On average, at a fixed 1/2,000 rate, you'll need around 2,000 casts per relic — but RNG being RNG, some players report pulling one in a few hundred casts while others grind for hours. Both outcomes are normal.

## What Is the Crested Relic Used For?

Here's where the relic earns its reputation. The Crested Relic's primary purpose isn't selling — it's **enchanting**.

### The Keepers Altar and Crested Enchantment

Take your Crested Relic to the **Keepers Altar** to enchant a rod with the **Crested Secondary Enchantment**. The standout effect of Crested is that it **notably slows hooked fish**, making them dramatically easier to reel in.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Enchantment Name | Crested |
| Type | Secondary Enchantment |
| Effect | Notably slows hooked fish |
| Applied At | Keepers Altar |
| Cost | 1 Crested Relic |

Why does slowing hooked fish matter? In Fisch, the hardest part of landing legendary and mythical-tier catches is managing the fish's erratic movement and line tension. A slower fish means:

- **Less line strain** — fewer snapped lines on big pulls
- **Easier reeling rhythm** — especially on low-stamina rods
- **Better success rate** on rare fish you'd otherwise lose

For players hunting endgame catches, that reliability can be worth far more than the relic's 12,000 C$ sell value. Community reports frequently describe Crested as a "quality of life" enchantment that turns frustrating loses into consistent catches.

### Should You Sell It Instead?

| Option | Value | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enchant a rod | Crested enchantment (functional power) | Best for most players |
| Sell it | ~12,000 C$ at base weight | Only if you already have Crested or need quick cash |

Unless you're early-game and desperate for C$, hold onto your first Crested Relic. The enchantment's utility scales with how long you play.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the drop rate for the Fisch Crested Relic?

The Fisch Crested Relic has a **fixed 1 in 2,000 catch chance** from any body of water. Crucially, this rate is not affected by luck stat, bait, weather, time, or season — it's one of the few rares in Fisch with a completely flat drop rate.

### Where can I use the Crested Relic?

Take it to the **Keepers Altar** to apply the Crested Secondary Enchantment to a rod of your choice. The Crested enchantment notably slows hooked fish, making difficult catches much easier to land.

### Is the Crested enchantment worth it?

For most players, yes — especially if you hunt rare or aggressive fish that frequently snap your line. Community reports highlight it as one of the more practical enchantments for consistency. If you're only catching common fish, the benefit is less noticeable, and selling the relic for ~12,000 C$ may make more sense early on.

### Can luck boosts or events help me get the Crested Relic faster?

Unfortunately, no. Per the official wiki, the Crested Relic's catch chance is not affected in any way by luck. Your best approach is simply maximizing the number of casts you make per session with a fast, reliable rod.
