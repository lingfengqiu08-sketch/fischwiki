## What Is the Sovereign Relic in Fisch?

The **Fisch Sovereign Relic** is a rare, regionless catch used to place powerful Sovereign enchantments on fishing rods. Getting a **Fisch Sovereign Relic** matters because its enchantments can improve control, resilience, progress speed, mutation odds, or valuable cosmetic traits. Although it can technically appear in any body of water, targeting a Sovereign Beam Pool during the correct event is dramatically more efficient.

This ancient stone is connected to the Keepers and the Statue of Sovereignty. Its practical purpose is more important than its lore: when a power burst is active at the Keepers Altar, the relic can help bind a Sovereign enchantment to a rod.

Here are the relic’s core statistics:

| Attribute | Sovereign Relic statistic |
|---|---:|
| Classification | Relic |
| Location category | Regionless |
| Standard catch chance | 1 in 10,000 |
| Standard percentage chance | 0.01% |
| Base weight | 75 kg |
| Base sale value | 30,000 C$ |
| Catch XP | 15,000 XP |
| Weather preference | None |
| Time preference | None |
| Season preference | None |
| Bait preference | None |

The standard catch probability is fixed and is not improved by ordinary Luck. That distinction is important: stacking generic Luck bonuses while fishing randomly is not an effective way to target this relic.

For current item records and future balance changes, consult the [Official Fisch Wiki’s Sovereign Relic entry](https://fischipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_Relic).

## How to Get a Fisch Sovereign Relic

There are three principal ways to obtain the relic: catch it anywhere at its extremely low global rate, fish inside a Sovereign Beam Pool during a qualifying event, or buy it when an eligible merchant offers it.

| Method | Chance or price | Requirements | Efficiency |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Any body of water | 1/10,000, or 0.01% | No special event | Very low |
| Sovereign Beam Pool | 1/150, or about 0.67% | Qualifying Sovereign event | High |
| Beam Pool with one relevant bonus | 1/75, or about 1.33% | Relic Construct Companion or applicable Mastery Enhancement | Very high |
| Beam Pool with both bonuses | 1/39, or about 2.56% | Both listed bonuses active | Best fishing rate |
| Rare Goods Dealer | 5,000 S$ | Item must be available | Guaranteed purchase |
| Rotating or weekly shop | Rotation-dependent | Check current inventory | Update-dependent |

A Sovereign Beam Pool can appear during the following events:

- **Sovereign Surge**
- **Sovereign Storm**
- **Sovereign Reckoning**

Fishing in one of these pools changes the base rate from 1 in 10,000 to approximately 1 in 150. That makes an event pool roughly 66.7 times more effective per catch than ordinary water before applying specialized bonuses.

### Improving the event-pool drop rate

Two targeted bonuses can improve the pool’s rate:

1. The **Relic Construct Companion**
2. The **Rod of the Exalted One’s Mastery Enhancement**

Using either one improves the chance to approximately 1 in 75. Combining both reportedly produces a rate near 1 in 39.

These are specialized improvements rather than ordinary Luck bonuses. If your goal is specifically to farm the **Fisch Sovereign Relic**, prioritize them over general-purpose Luck equipment.

Merchant availability may change between updates. The recorded direct price is 5,000 S$ from the Rare Goods Dealer, while the item’s update history also notes availability through Kaito’s Weekly Shop. Check the current shop interface before planning around either purchase route.

## Best Farming Strategy and Expected Catch Counts

The most efficient strategy is to prepare before a Sovereign event begins, travel to the Sovereign Beam Pool immediately, and maximize your number of completed catches while the pool remains available.

A 1-in-39 rate does not guarantee one relic every 39 catches. Each catch is another probability roll, so unlucky streaks remain possible. The figures below estimate how many independent catches are required to reach approximately a 50% or 95% cumulative chance of receiving at least one relic.

| Setup | Chance per catch | Catches for about 50% success | Catches for about 95% success |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Random body of water | 0.01% | 6,932 | 29,956 |
| Standard Sovereign Beam Pool | 0.67% | 104 | 449 |
| Pool with one bonus | 1.33% | 52 | 224 |
| Pool with both bonuses | 2.56% | 27 | 116 |

These estimates use the probability formula `1 − (1 − p)^n`, where `p` is the drop chance and `n` is the number of catches. They describe cumulative probability, not a pity system or guaranteed threshold.

### Recommended preparation checklist

Before farming the **Fisch Sovereign Relic**, complete as many of these steps as possible:

- Equip the Relic Construct Companion if it is available to you.
- Use the Rod of the Exalted One with the relevant Mastery Enhancement.
- Clear unnecessary items from your inventory.
- Position yourself where you can reach the event pool quickly.
- Favor reliable catch speed over unnecessary generic Luck.
- Avoid distractions while the limited pool is active.
- Save enough S$ in case the relic appears in a merchant rotation.

Because the relic has no weather, season, time, or bait preference, you do not need to wait for another environmental condition after the Sovereign event begins. Your main constraints are pool availability, catch speed, and access to the two targeted rate improvements.

### Choosing between fishing and buying

Your best acquisition route depends on what you already own.

| Player situation | Recommended approach | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Newer player without specialized bonuses | Monitor merchants and fish event pools | Random-water farming is too inefficient |
| Player with one rate bonus | Farm every available Beam Pool | The 1/75 rate is practical |
| Player with both bonuses | Prioritize active event farming | The 1/39 rate is the best listed catch chance |
| Player with excess S$ | Purchase when available | Removes drop-rate uncertainty |
| Player fishing outside an event | Treat the relic as a surprise bonus | The global 0.01% rate is not target-friendly |

Community reports may describe short lucky sessions or very long dry streaks. Those outcomes are normal under random drop systems and do not prove that the published rate has changed.

## How to Use the Relic at the Keepers Altar

The primary reason to keep a **Fisch Sovereign Relic** is rod enchanting. It can be used at the Keepers Altar while a power burst is active, with relics placed into the altar’s pillars determining which group of Sovereign enchantments can roll.

A particular Sovereign enchantment type has an initial listed chance of approximately one in three, or 33.3%. If a rod already has that same Sovereign enchantment type and you reroll it, the chance rises to approximately one in two, or 50%.

| Enchanting situation | Listed chance | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| Initial roll for a specific Sovereign type | 1/3, or about 33.3% | The desired result is not guaranteed |
| Rerolling the same type already on the rod | 1/2, or about 50% | Better odds, but failure remains possible |
| Trying to access another Sovereign type | Combination-dependent | Change the relic inputs placed in the pillars |

Two documented Sovereign enchantments are especially notable.

| Enchantment | Effects | Best suited for |
|---|---|---|
| Steady Crown | +25% Resilience, +0.1 Control, +25% Progress Speed | Difficult catches and general consistency |
| Glimmering Crown | +10% Shiny/Sparkling chance, listed as 1.85×, and +20% chance for all mutations | Collectors and mutation-focused farming |

**Steady Crown** is the practical option when you want a rod to handle difficult catches more comfortably. Resilience helps stabilize the fishing encounter, additional control improves bar management, and progress speed shortens successful catches.

**Glimmering Crown** is oriented toward rare variants rather than catch stability. It is more attractive when your goal is finding Shiny, Sparkling, or mutated fish for collections and high-value catches.

Relic combinations matter at the altar. The available record identifies an Enchant Relic paired with a Sovereign Relic for the documented Sovereign enchanting setup, but the exact result still depends on the eligible roll pool and current game version. Confirm the altar preview before consuming rare materials.

### Practical enchanting advice

Do not use your only **Fisch Sovereign Relic** impulsively. Before activating the altar:

1. Decide which rod will receive the enchantment.
2. Confirm that a power burst is active.
3. Verify the relics placed in each pillar.
4. Check which enchantment family the setup can produce.
5. Accept that the first roll may not be your preferred result.
6. Keep extra relics if you intend to reroll.

If a rod already performs well and is expensive to replace, Steady Crown can provide broad, predictable utility. Glimmering Crown is more specialized and becomes more valuable when variant hunting is your primary endgame activity.

## Should You Sell or Save the Sovereign Relic?

At its base weight, the relic sells for 30,000 C$ and awards 15,000 XP when caught. Those are meaningful rewards, but the relic’s enchanting utility can be harder to replace than its sale value.

For most established players, saving at least one relic is the safer choice.

| Decision | When it makes sense | Main drawback |
|---|---|---|
| Keep it for enchanting | You have a strong rod or plan to use the Keepers Altar | No immediate currency |
| Save multiple copies | You expect to reroll Sovereign enchantments | Uses inventory space and delays profit |
| Sell it for 30,000 C$ base value | You urgently need currency and already have extras | Reacquiring it may take time |
| Buy it for 5,000 S$ | You value certainty and have spare premium shop currency | Opportunity cost of spending S$ |
| Hold it as a collector item | You collect rare relics or update-limited resources | No immediate gameplay return |

A simple rule is to keep your first one, reserve additional copies for rerolls, and sell only the surplus. This approach protects you from needing to repeat an event grind when you eventually unlock or upgrade a suitable rod.

The relic’s change history also shows that acquisition options and effects can evolve. It was introduced in version 1.83.0, later received its passive classification, appeared in special-event content, and gained another shop source in version 2.0. A temporary Divine Protection association was added and subsequently removed, demonstrating why old guides may contain outdated claims.

## Fisch Sovereign Relic FAQ

### Can Luck increase the Fisch Sovereign Relic drop rate?

Ordinary Luck does not affect its fixed global catch chance. To improve your odds, fish in a Sovereign Beam Pool and use the Relic Construct Companion, the Rod of the Exalted One’s relevant Mastery Enhancement, or both.

### Where is the best place to catch a Sovereign Relic?

The best location is a Sovereign Beam Pool during Sovereign Surge, Sovereign Storm, or Sovereign Reckoning. The pool starts at approximately a 1-in-150 rate, compared with only 1 in 10,000 in ordinary water.

### What is the best enchantment from the relic?

It depends on your objective. Steady Crown is better for catch consistency because it adds resilience, control, and progress speed. Glimmering Crown is generally better for players hunting Shiny, Sparkling, and mutated catches.

### Is the Fisch Sovereign Relic worth selling?

Usually, you should keep at least one. Its base 30,000 C$ sale value is useful, but Sovereign enchanting and rerolling can require multiple copies. Selling becomes more reasonable after you have completed your desired rod setup or accumulated extras.
