Hidden beneath the ruins of The Depths, **Fisch Crystal Cove** combines fishing, exploration, an expensive rod, and a secret underground venue in one easy-to-miss location. Visiting **Fisch Crystal Cove** matters if you want to complete its eight-entry bestiary, earn 30,000 C$, or collect the exclusive Bloxy Cola Bobber. Knowing the right coordinates also saves you from searching every tunnel and rampart in The Depths.

This guide explains where to find the entrance, what to bring, how to open the Underground Music Venue, and which fishing conditions are worth targeting. Information reflects the location’s documented state as of September 23, 2026, so older videos may show content—particularly The Chapel—that is no longer accessible.

## Fisch Crystal Cove Location and Quick Facts

Crystal Cove is a sublocation within The Depths in the First Sea. Its entrance is built into a rampart among the area's ruins rather than positioned near the cove’s central fishing zone.

That distinction matters because entering the destination and navigating to its points of interest require different coordinates.

| Point of interest | GPS coordinates | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Crystal Cove entrance | `1302, -701, 1604` | Tunnel entrance on a rampart in The Depths |
| General Crystal Cove area | `1364, -612, 2472` | Main destination coordinates |
| Underground Music Venue trapdoor | `1350, -604, 2329` | Secret venue entrance after unlocking it |
| Crystal Crasher | `1341, -580, 2369` | Rod purchase location on a nearby ledge |

The negative vertical coordinates indicate that the area sits far below the ordinary surface level. If your GPS shows the correct X and Z values but your Y coordinate is much higher, you are probably above the tunnel rather than inside it.

Crystal Cove currently offers the following major activities:

- A regional fishing pool with 11 possible catches
- An eight-entry bestiary
- The Crystal Crasher rod for 2,400,000 C$
- The Drowned NPC
- An Inn Keeper
- A hidden Underground Music Venue
- The remains of content associated with the retired Chapel

Players can access Fisch through the official [Fisch experience page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/16732694052/Fisch).

## How to Find and Explore Fisch Crystal Cove

Start by reaching The Depths, then move toward the ruined ramparts. The relevant tunnel entrance is around `1302, -701, 1604`. Do not navigate directly toward the cove’s general coordinates before finding this passage; the surrounding terrain can leave you on the wrong side of a wall or at an incorrect elevation.

Use this route as a practical checklist:

| Step | Action | Coordinate or item | Common issue |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Travel into The Depths | Area access | Arriving without enough time to explore |
| 2 | Locate the ruined ramparts | Near the entrance coordinate | Searching at the floor of the ruins |
| 3 | Find the tunnel opening | `1302, -701, 1604` | Standing above or below the correct elevation |
| 4 | Follow the tunnel into the cove | Toward `1364, -612, 2472` | Turning back before reaching the larger chamber |
| 5 | Activate GPS or note landmarks | GPS recommended | Losing the exit after exploring side areas |
| 6 | Mark the rear shelter | `1350, -604, 2329` | Missing the venue’s concealed trapdoor |

The best way to avoid navigation problems is to compare all three coordinate values. Matching only X and Z is not enough in a vertically layered area like The Depths.

### What to bring

A short preparation trip can prevent an unnecessary return journey. Consider carrying:

- A capable fishing rod for bestiary attempts
- Insect bait for the Opalescent Catfish
- A Chaotic Enchant Relic for the Underground Music Venue
- At least 2,400,000 C$ if you intend to buy the Crystal Crasher
- Equipment or utilities you normally use to navigate The Depths
- Enough inventory space for repeated catches

If you have exactly 2.4 million C$, think carefully before spending all of it. The available reference data confirms the Crystal Crasher’s price and purpose, but it does not provide enough verified statistics to compare its performance with every alternative rod. Purchase it for collection or progression only after checking its current in-game attributes.

## Crystal Crasher, NPCs, and the Underground Music Venue

The Crystal Crasher is the only documented item sold directly within Fisch Crystal Cove. It costs **2,400,000 C$** and can be found on a ledge close to the Underground Music Venue entrance at approximately `1341, -580, 2369`.

| Crystal Crasher | 2,400,000 C$ | Ledge at `1341, -580, 2369` | Obtain a fishing rod |

### Unlocking the Underground Music Venue

To open the venue, place a **Chaotic Enchant Relic** on its associated pedestal. This action opens a trapdoor inside a shelter toward the back of the cove. The shelter is near `1350, -604, 2329`.

A standard Enchant Relic is not interchangeable with the required mutated relic. Check that the item explicitly has the **Chaotic** mutation before traveling. If the trapdoor remains closed, verify the following:

1. You are at the rear shelter rather than the Crystal Crasher ledge.
2. You brought an Enchant Relic with the Chaotic mutation.
3. You placed it on the intended pedestal.
4. You checked the trapdoor after completing the interaction.
5. You are using current instructions rather than a guide for the former Chapel.

Community reports and player experience may help identify temporary bugs after an update, but they should not replace the visible item name and current in-game prompts.

## Fishing and Completing the Crystal Cove Bestiary

There are **11 total catches** associated with Crystal Cove, while only **eight fish count toward its regional bestiary**. That means catching everything in the water is not identical to finishing the bestiary.

Two documented non-bestiary-style catches include Destroyed Fossil and Scrap Metal. Both favor daytime fishing, although listed preferences improve the odds rather than acting as strict requirements.

| Catch | Preferred weather | Preferred time | Preferred season | Preferred bait | Average weight | Average value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Destroyed Fossil | Any | Day | Any | None listed | 2.75 kg | 21.4 C$ |
| Scrap Metal | Any | Day | Any | None listed | 3 kg | 24 C$ |
| Mutated Crystal Shrimp | Rain | Night | Summer | None listed | 37 kg | 226.9 C$ |
| Opalescent Catfish | Clear | Any | Winter or Spring | Insect | 3.9 kg | 219.5 C$ |

The Mutated Crystal Shrimp and Opalescent Catfish are confirmed bestiary entries. Their preferences suggest two distinct fishing sessions rather than one all-purpose visit.

### Recommended fishing schedule

| Target session | Best conditions | Suggested preparation | Main target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daytime cleanup | Day, any season | General-purpose setup | Destroyed Fossil and Scrap Metal |
| Summer storm session | Rain, night, summer | Luck-focused setup | Mutated Crystal Shrimp |
| Clear seasonal session | Clear weather, winter or spring | Insect bait | Opalescent Catfish |
| General completion | Conditions based on missing entries | Review the regional bestiary | Any uncaught Crystal Cove fish |

Preferences are probability modifiers, not mandatory spawning conditions. You may catch a fish outside its ideal setup, but aligning weather, season, time, and bait should make a long grind more efficient.

Version 1.65.0 corrected Crystal Cove so that Luck would be respected properly. Consequently, current players should benefit from Luck-based equipment and boosts as intended. Advice from before that patch may underestimate the usefulness of Luck in this location.

Completing the eight-entry Fisch Crystal Cove bestiary awards:

- **30,000 C$**
- **Bloxy Cola Bobber**

For completion-focused players, the bobber is likely the more distinctive reward because currency can be earned through many activities. Before leaving, open the regional bestiary and confirm that all eight slots are registered; catching 11 objects or fish does not automatically prove completion.

## The Chapel: Retired Content and Old Relic Requirements

The Chapel is no longer available in the current documented version of Crystal Cove. It was a former quest area reached by offering specially mutated Enchant Relics to a waterfall.

This retired content is important because older guides may still instruct players to search for five NPCs, five rod-skin pedestals, or a Chapel passage. Those directions describe historical gameplay and should not be confused with the active Underground Music Venue puzzle.

| Former Chapel element | Historical requirement or function | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Waterfall entrance | Offer an accepted mutated Enchant Relic | Unavailable |
| Accepted mutations | King’s Blessing, Husk, Fabulous, or Mastered | Historical |
| Ascended relic | Worked only under certain progression conditions | Historical |
| Quest NPCs | Five NPCs with corresponding questlines | Unavailable |
| Rod-skin pedestals | Displayed rewards from NPC quests | Unavailable |
| Polyhistor questline | Required the other questlines first | Unavailable |

Two documented historical quest examples were:

| NPC | Former prerequisites | Former reward |
|---|---|---|
| Tempest | King’s Blessing Enchant Relic and Poseidon Rod | Caeruleus Reliquiarium skin |
| Alexandria | Fabulous Enchant Relic and Fabulous Rod | Capella Vitrea skin |

The old waterfall used different messages depending on what the player offered. An unsupported mutation indicated that the relic had not been permanently changed, while holding the wrong item produced a message explaining that the water did not recognize it. An Ascended relic could also be rejected when the player had unfinished progression.

These details are useful for interpreting archived videos, but they are not a current method for entering The Chapel. The area may return in a future update, yet no return should be assumed until it appears in the live game.

Do not confuse the old and new relic interactions:

| Objective | Relic needed | Interaction point | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enter the former Chapel | Several specific mutations were formerly accepted | Waterfall | No longer available |
| Open the Underground Music Venue | Chaotic Enchant Relic | Pedestal near rear shelter | Available |
| Buy the Crystal Crasher | No relic documented | Ledge beside venue area | Available |

## FAQ About Fisch Crystal Cove

### Where is Fisch Crystal Cove?

Fisch Crystal Cove is inside a tunnel in the ruins of The Depths. Look for the entrance on a rampart near GPS coordinates `1302, -701, 1604`. The general cove coordinates are approximately `1364, -612, 2472`.

### How do I unlock the Underground Music Venue?

Bring a Chaotic Enchant Relic and place it on the appropriate pedestal. A trapdoor will open in the shelter at the rear of the cove, near `1350, -604, 2329`. The venue puzzle is separate from the retired Chapel entrance.

### What do I get for completing the Crystal Cove bestiary?

Completing all eight entries rewards **30,000 C$** and the **Bloxy Cola Bobber**. Crystal Cove has 11 possible catches overall, but only eight belong to its bestiary.

### Can I still enter The Chapel in Fisch Crystal Cove?

No. The Chapel is documented as unavailable as of September 23, 2026. Its mutated-relic quests and rod-skin rewards belong to past content, although the location could potentially return in a later update.
