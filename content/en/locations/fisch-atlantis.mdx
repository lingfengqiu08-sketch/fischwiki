Fisch Atlantis is a sprawling late-game region packed with puzzle chambers, specialized fishing pools, expensive rods, and exclusive events. Learning its layout matters because one wrong clock answer, missing teammate, or forgotten piece of diving gear can end a run. This Fisch Atlantis guide explains how to enter the city, solve every major trial, unlock the Kraken Lair, and prioritize your purchases.

## Fisch Atlantis Location, Entrance, and Preparation

Atlantis is beneath the First Sea’s Ocean, with its central area positioned around **GPS -4300, -580, 1800**. However, you do not need to sail directly to those underwater coordinates.

The practical entrance is a crevice inside the central island at Grand Reef. Enter the opening at approximately **GPS -3576, 148, 524**, and the game will transport you to the Atlantis hub. An Inn Keeper and a portal back to Moosewood are available near the arrival point.

The Heart of Zeus was once required to enter, but that requirement was removed in version 1.79. You can now use the Grand Reef crevice without completing the old Forsaken Shores lever and TNT sequence.

Before visiting, confirm that you are playing the current version through the [official Fisch experience on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/16732694052/Fisch).

It involves activating five levers at Forsaken Shores, collecting TNT from the island’s skull, and using it to blast open an underwater passage beneath Grand Reef. Paying Captain Neptune 10,000C$ for information is optional rather than a required step.

## Atlantis Sub-Locations and Progression Order

Four primary sub-locations can be approached in any order. The fifth, Kraken Lair, is the finale and remains blocked until you solve the Mythological Clocks in the other four areas.

| Area | GPS | Main challenge | Clock answer | Major reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sunken Depths | -4606, -594, 1843 | Symbol-order puzzle | Blue Lyre | Tempest Rod |
| Ethereal Abyss | -4107, -603, 1823 | Two-player timed rooms | Orange Helmet | Abyssal Specter Rod |
| Poseidon Temple | -4299, -604, 1587 | Six-fish pedestal offering | Black Scythe | Poseidon Rod and Wrath Totem |
| Zeus Trial | -4295, -583, 2021 | Two trials within five minutes | Purple Moon | Zeus Rod and Storm Totem |
| Kraken Lair | -4295, -991, 1792 | Complete four earlier clocks | Red Spear | Kraken Rod and Kraken Hunts |

The clock riddles are not forgiving. Entering an incorrect answer twice causes Kraken Tentacles to kill your character, so verify the symbol before confirming it.

A sensible route for most groups is:

1. Complete Sunken Depths to learn how the clocks work.
2. Bring a second player to Ethereal Abyss.
3. Solve Zeus Trial while your group is assembled.
4. Open Poseidon Temple after gathering all six required fish.
5. Descend to Kraken Lair and activate the final clock.

Poseidon Temple may take the longest to prepare because its fish are consumed. If you already have the required sharks, completing it earlier is reasonable.

## Every Fisch Atlantis Puzzle Solution

Each section of Fisch Atlantis uses a different mechanic. Some challenges test memory, while others require coordination, platforming, or valuable fish.

### Sunken Depths symbol puzzle

The Sunken Trial is designed for two players, although it can be completed alone. One person normally enters the underwater maze while the other waits beside the entrance panel.

Swim to the Sunken Sigil at approximately **-5167, -680, 1710**. Interact with its five symbols in any order, but write down the exact sequence you selected. Return to the entrance and press those symbols on the code panel in the same order.

| Step | Action | Practical tip |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Equip diving gear and enter the maze | Super Flippers reduce oxygen pressure |
| 2 | Locate the Sunken Sigil | Use GPS -5167, -680, 1710 |
| 3 | Press all five symbols | Screenshot or record the order |
| 4 | Return to the entrance panel | Avoid guessing from memory |
| 5 | Repeat the same sequence | The door opens after a correct entry |
| 6 | Solve the clock | Choose the Blue Lyre |

Inside, you can access a fishing pool and buy the **Tempest Rod for 100,000C$**.

### Ethereal Abyss two-player challenge

Ethereal Abyss requires two cooperating players. Advanced Diving Gear is strongly recommended because the challenge combines oxygen management with a strict time limit.

One player enters the left room and the other enters the right. Once both are inside, the doors close and a **90-second timer** begins. The team must complete the paired challenge three consecutive times. Failing a round means starting another sequence of three successful rounds.

Community reports often identify communication—not movement speed—as the main source of failure. Use voice chat or short, agreed-upon text callouts before entering.

After opening the main chamber:

- Purchase the **Abyssal Specter Rod for 300,000C$** if desired.
- Find the Mythological Clock.
- Select the **Orange Helmet**, corresponding to the Goddess of War clue.

### Poseidon Temple fish pedestals

Poseidon Temple’s clock is accessible behind Poseidon’s chair. Choose the **Black Scythe** for the “King of Titans” clue.

The separate reward chamber requires six specific fish divided between two rooms:

| Room | Left pedestal | Center pedestal | Right pedestal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left room | Great Hammerhead Shark | Whale Shark | Icebeard Shark |
| Right room | Great White Shark | Ancient Megalodon | Ginsu Shark |

Every submitted fish is consumed. Progress is tied to the server and resets if you leave, so gather all six before beginning. Other players can contribute fish, and nearby players may receive access when the server-side door opens.

Beyond the door, you will find:

- **Poseidon Rod:** 450,000C$
- **Poseidon Wrath Totem:** 150,000C$
- Poseidon Temple’s specialized fishing area

Using a Poseidon Wrath Totem turns the water pink, enables the Wrath mutation, and makes catches more difficult.

### Zeus Trial and eight-digit code

The Zeus Trial gives you **five minutes** to complete two challenges. Gliders are disabled, so normal movement and careful timing are required.

The left path is a red-light, green-light game. Move while the Zeus statue is turned away and stop before it faces you. Reaching the end reveals a four-digit code.

The right path is a lava obstacle course with real and fake stones. Falling returns you to the beginning, but attempted fake stones help expose the usable route for the next run. Completing this side provides another four-digit code.

Record both numbers immediately and combine them at the upstairs keypad to produce the required eight-digit entry. The exact digits are generated during the trials rather than being a universal code.

Inside Zeus’s Sanctuary, select the **Purple Moon** at the clock. You can also purchase the **Zeus Storm Totem for 150,000C$** and the **Zeus Rod for 500,000C$**.

### Kraken Lair and final clock

Kraken Lair is directly below the Atlantis hub at approximately **-4295, -991, 1792**. Its four colored lights correspond to the previously completed regions.

Once all four outer Mythological Clocks are active, approach the Lair’s final clock and choose the **Red Spear** for the “Ruler of the Underworld” clue. This opens the grate leading into a deeper swimming tunnel.

At the end, you gain access to:

- The Kraken Lair fishing pool
- **Kraken Rod for 950,000C$**
- Kraken Hunt participation
- The final stage of core Fisch Atlantis progression

Bring diving equipment even if you have already unlocked the door. The descent and final tunnel still involve limited oxygen.

## Efficient Route and Mistakes to Avoid

A coordinated group can reduce repeat trips by preparing its gear, currency, and sacrificial fish before entering Fisch Atlantis.

Use this checklist:

- Buy Advanced Diving Gear and Super Flippers first.
- Save every required Poseidon Temple fish in advance.
- Bring a dependable partner for Ethereal Abyss.
- Screenshot Sunken Depths symbols and Zeus Trial codes.
- Complete each Mythological Clock before leaving its chamber.
- Do not guess clock answers; two mistakes are fatal.
- Carry a Conception Conch as an inexpensive escape option.
- Reserve 300,000C$ if you want both event totems.
- Budget 2,300,000C$ for all five trial-area rods.
- Activate the nearby Inn Keeper to simplify return visits.

For a first run, focus on access rather than buying everything. Unlock each area, solve its clock, and reach Kraken Lair. You can return later with enough money to purchase rods or complete each bestiary under favorable weather and time conditions.

A deeper opening leading toward Wrath of Olympus can also be found around **-4295, -610, 1810**. Treat that route as additional progression rather than one of the five core Atlantis clock areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Fisch Atlantis

### How do you enter Fisch Atlantis?

Travel to the Grand Reef’s central island and enter the crevice near **GPS -3576, 148, 524**. It teleports you to the Atlantis hub. The Heart of Zeus is no longer required for entry.

### Can you complete Atlantis solo?

You can complete parts of Fisch Atlantis alone, including the Sunken Depths puzzle. Ethereal Abyss is built around two players in separate rooms, however, so bring a partner for reliable full progression.

### What are all five Mythological Clock answers?

The answers are **Blue Lyre** in Sunken Depths, **Orange Helmet** in Ethereal Abyss, **Black Scythe** in Poseidon Temple, **Purple Moon** in Zeus’s Sanctuary, and **Red Spear** in Kraken Lair.

### How much do the main Atlantis rods cost?

The five puzzle-area rods cost **2,300,000C$ combined**: Tempest Rod at 100,000C$, Abyssal Specter Rod at 300,000C$, Poseidon Rod at 450,000C$, Zeus Rod at 500,000C$, and Kraken Rod at 950,000C$.
