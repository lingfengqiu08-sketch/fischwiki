## Why Your Rod Choice Matters in Fisch

In Fisch, your rod is the single most important piece of gear you'll ever own. It determines how quickly you hook fish, what you can actually reel in, and how much money every fishing session puts in your pocket. That's why smart Fisch rod progression matters — knowing which rod to grab next saves you hours of wasted grinding and millions of wasted C$.

The good news? The upgrade path is well-established by the community. This guide walks you through the four phases of Fisch rod progression — early game, mid game, late game, and endgame — with full stat tables and the passives that make each rod worth chasing.

One quick note before we cast off: no single rod is "perfect." Every rod trades off speed, luck, control, and resilience. Pick based on your goals — farming money, hunting rare fish, or grinding XP.

## Early Game: Ditch the Flimsy Rod Fast

Everyone starts with the Flimsy Rod, and it's exactly what the name implies. It barely handles 10.4kg and every single stat sits at 0%. It gets you on the water, but not much else.

Here's a tip based on widespread player experience: **skip the Training Rod.** The upgrade is so small you'll outgrow it within an hour of playtime. That's money and effort better spent elsewhere.

### Fungal Rod — Your First Real Upgrade

Head to Mushgrove Swamp and speak to Agaric, who tasks you with catching an Alligator. Complete it and the Fungal Rod is yours. Its passive is fantastic for beginners: every cast has a 60% chance to grant a luck boost lasting 45 seconds.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Lure Speed | -10% |
| Luck | 45% |
| Control | 0 |
| Resilience | 0% |
| Max Kg | 200kg |

### Arctic Rod — The Early Game Powerhouse

Once you've banked around 25,000C$, buy the Arctic Rod at the Northern Summit. It applies the Frozen Mutation (x1.3 value) to catches, and with a 7,500kg cap, it can handle far heavier fish than anything else at this stage.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost | 25,000C$ |
| Lure Speed | 25% |
| Luck | 45% |
| Control | 0.06 |
| Resilience | 15% |
| Max Kg | 7,500kg |

This is the rod that turns early grinding into consistent profit. According to [Roblox's official Fisch page](https://www.roblox.com/games/13491874516/Fisch), the game receives frequent content updates, so rod availability can shift — but the Arctic Rod has remained a community staple.

## Mid Game: Passives Start Paying Off

Mid game is where rods stop being simple stat sticks and start printing money through mutations.

### Trident Rod — Atlantean Power

Sold at Trident's Temple for 150,000C$, the Trident Rod has a 30% chance to apply the Atlantean Mutation, tripling a fish's sell value. With 150% Luck, it's one of the most balanced mid-game options for both rarity hunting and cash farming.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Lure Speed | 25% |
| Luck | 150% |
| Control | 0.05 |
| Resilience | 0% |
| Max Kg | 6,000kg |

### Sunken Rod — The Treasure Hunter's Pick

You can't buy the Sunken Rod — it's found in Treasure Chests, which makes it a favorite among explorers. Its passives are excellent:

- 25% chance to reel in a Treasure Map every 10 catches
- 10% chance for the Sunken Mutation, selling fish at **8.25x** normal value

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Lure Speed | 30% |
| Luck | 150% |
| Control | 0.15 |
| Resilience | 15% |
| Max Kg | 5,000kg |

If you enjoy exploration as much as fishing, this is the mid-game rod for you.

Costing 750,000C$ from inside The Depths, this rod rewards consistency: every 3 successful catches earns you a bonus fish. Community reports consistently rank it among the best rods for farming both XP and currency simultaneously.

## Endgame: The Underground Music Venue Questlines

Endgame rods come from questlines at the Underground Music Venue rather than straight purchases — and they're the strongest gear in the game.

### Chrysalis Rod — Stability Meets Profit

- +10% Progress Speed
- 90% chance for Bloom Mutation (x5 value)
- 10% chance for Flora Mutation (x10 value)
- 20% chance an Eye spawns an extra Flora fish (20% chance it's shiny/sparkling)

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Lure Speed | 100% |
| Luck | 150% |
| Control | 0 |
| Resilience | 100% |

### Wingripper — The Absolute Apex

The Wingripper is the crown jewel of Fisch rod progression. Its day/night split mechanics create genuine gameplay decisions:

- +22% Progress Speed by day; **+222%** at night
- 0.22 Control by day, -0.2 at night
- 30% chance for Diurnal Mutation (x13.5 value)
- 10% chance for Nocturnal Mutation (x16.2 value)

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Lure Speed | 222% |
| Luck | 222% |
| Control | 0.22 |
| Resilience | 22% |
| Max Kg | Infinite |

Fishing at night with the Wingripper is wildly powerful — just beware the negative Control, which makes reeling trickier.

## FAQ

**What is the best rod in Fisch right now?**
The Wingripper is widely considered the best endgame rod, thanks to its 222% Lure Speed and Luck, infinite max weight, and mutations worth up to 16.2x normal value.

**Is the Training Rod worth buying?**
Most players skip it. The stat gain is minimal, and you'll outgrow it within an hour. Go straight from the Flimsy Rod to the Fungal Rod.

**Which rod makes the most money in Fisch?**
The Kraken Rod and Wingripper lead for profit thanks to their high-value mutations, but the Sunken Rod offers the best budget option at 8.25x sell value.

**How does Fisch rod progression work for new players?**
Follow this path: Flimsy → Fungal Rod (free quest) → Arctic Rod (25,000C$) → Trident or Sunken Rod → Rod of The Depths → Kraken Rod → endgame questline rods at the Underground Music Venue.
