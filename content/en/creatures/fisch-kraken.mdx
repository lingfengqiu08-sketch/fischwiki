The **Fisch Kraken** is one of the game’s most intimidating catches, combining Exotic rarity with an unusually punishing reeling encounter. Catching the **Fisch Kraken** matters because it awards 20,000 XP, can sell for tens of thousands of C$, and fills a major entry in the Kraken Lair bestiary. Success depends on finding or triggering a Kraken Hunt, arriving with suitable equipment, and surviving a locked progress penalty plus the Kraken’s disruptive fling ability.

This guide covers its location, spawn methods, catch statistics, rewards, and the practical techniques that can improve your chances.

## Fisch Kraken Overview and Key Statistics

The Kraken is an Exotic creature associated with Kraken Lair. It does not require a particular season or weather condition, so preparation should focus on hunt availability, luck, disturbance, and control during the fishing minigame.

Its defining catch modifier is a locked **-85% progress speed**. Most ordinary modifiers cannot remove or change that penalty, making the encounter substantially slower than a routine catch.

Luck favors the Kraken’s catch chance, but luck should not be confused with a guaranteed spawn. You still need access to the appropriate hunt or an eligible special event. Once hooked, luck also does not replace the mechanical control required to finish the reeling sequence.

The absence of weather and seasonal preferences is useful. Players do not have to wait for rain, fog, summer, winter, or another environmental combination before attempting a hunt. That makes the hunt trigger—not the forecast—the main access requirement.

## Where to Find and Spawn the Kraken

The primary place to target the Fisch Kraken is **Kraken Lair**. Kraken Hunts can appear naturally while players fish there with equipment that has a disturbance stat. The Kraken Rod is one documented example of a rod designed around this mechanic.

A **Kraken Hunt Totem** offers another method of initiating the activity. This can be preferable when you want a more deliberate attempt instead of repeatedly fishing while waiting for a hunt to occur naturally.

| Spawn method | Where it works | Main requirement | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural Kraken Hunt | Kraken Lair | Fish with a rod that has disturbance | Repeated farming sessions |
| Kraken Hunt Totem | Associated hunt area | Obtain and activate the appropriate totem | Planned attempts |
| Eligible Admin Event | Any body of water during the event | The specific event must be active | Opportunistic catches |

The Kraken has also been made available at a fixed chance in any body of water during certain Admin Events. Version history identifies **Ghastly Tide** and **Sunken Seas Storm** as events that have supported this method. The available reference data does not provide the numerical event catch chance, so any percentage claimed without current official confirmation should be treated cautiously.

Admin Events are not a dependable everyday farming strategy because players generally cannot activate them whenever they want. Check live announcements and the game’s current event status rather than assuming an older event is active. ## How to Prepare for a Fisch Kraken Catch

A good setup must solve two different problems: accessing the hunt and controlling the fish after it bites. A rod with disturbance can help with the first problem, but catch control remains vital because the Fisch Kraken applies a severe progress-speed reduction.

The source data confirms that the -85% modifier is locked against most modifier effects. Therefore, do not build a strategy around completely canceling it unless a current item description explicitly says it can bypass locked penalties.

Prioritize these characteristics:

- **Adequate weight capacity:** Even the smallest natural Kraken weighs 50,000 kg.
- **High resilience or control:** These qualities can make sudden movement easier to manage.
- **Useful luck:** The Kraken is an Exotic catch whose chance benefits from luck.
- **Disturbance:** Relevant when trying to generate natural Kraken Hunts in the lair.
- **Consistency:** A stable setup is often more valuable than a high-risk configuration during a lengthy reel.

## Countering the Kraken’s Fling Ability

The Fisch Kraken has a special attack called **fling** during the reeling minigame. On its initial attack state, each fish movement carries an 11% chance of triggering the effect.

When fling activates, it pushes the fishing bar, locks the player’s input toward either the left or right for one second, and drains 8% progress over 0.8 seconds. The ability then enters a six-second cooldown.

Its activation chance decreases by 1.1 percentage points after each attack until reaching a minimum of 5.5%.

| Attack stage | Fling chance per fish movement |
|---:|---:|
| Initial stage | 11.0% |
| After one attack | 9.9% |
| After two attacks | 8.8% |
| After three attacks | 7.7% |
| After four attacks | 6.6% |
| Floor | 5.5% |

The declining chance does not mean the encounter becomes harmless. Because the reel can last a long time, even a 5.5% chance per movement can create repeated disruptions.

### How to respond when fling triggers

1. **Do not overcorrect immediately.** Input is temporarily forced in one direction, so aggressive counter-input may send the bar too far once control returns.
2. **Watch the direction of displacement.** Prepare to guide the bar back toward the target as soon as the one-second lock ends.
3. **Expect progress loss.** The 8% drain is part of the attack; panicking usually causes additional losses.
4. **Use the cooldown as a recovery window.** After fling begins, the six-second cooldown gives you time to stabilize and rebuild progress.
5. **Favor controlled adjustments.** Short corrections are generally safer than holding one direction continuously.

Think of the fight as a recovery challenge rather than a speed challenge. The locked progress reduction means you cannot rely on rapidly filling the meter before another disruption. Instead, protect your existing progress, recover cleanly from each fling, and avoid turning one forced movement into multiple mistakes.

Player experience can vary with rod behavior, enchantments, device input, and network latency. Community reports about a “best” control method should therefore be treated as setup-specific advice, not a universal guarantee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do you catch the Fisch Kraken?

The Fisch Kraken is primarily found in Kraken Lair during a Kraken Hunt. Hunts can occur naturally while fishing there with a disturbance-capable rod, or they can be initiated with a Kraken Hunt Totem. Certain Admin Events have also allowed the Kraken to appear at a fixed chance in any body of water.

### Does the Kraken require specific weather, bait, or a season?

It has no listed weather or seasonal preference. The available data also does not identify a mandatory bait, so hunt access, luck, equipment capacity, and minigame control are more important than waiting for a particular forecast.

### Why is the Kraken so difficult to reel in?

It has a locked -85% progress-speed modifier and can use fling. Fling temporarily forces directional input, removes 8% progress over 0.8 seconds, and may occur again after its six-second cooldown. The combination creates a slow encounter in which recovery and precision matter more than speed.

### How much are the XP and sale rewards?

Catching the Kraken grants 20,000 XP. A minimum-weight specimen is worth about 10,416.7C$, the true average is approximately 23,958.3C$, and a maximum natural-weight catch can sell for about 78,750C$.
