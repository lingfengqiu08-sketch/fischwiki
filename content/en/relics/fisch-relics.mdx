## Why Fisch Relics Matter More Than You Think

If you've spent any time grinding in Roblox's Fisch, you already know that Enchant Relics are the backbone of endgame progression. Fisch relics are rare items used to enchant your fishing rods at the Keeper's Altar, and without a steady supply, you'll be stuck with mediocre rod rolls while other players reel in legendary catches. Beyond enchanting, relics are also gatekeepers for some of the best rods in the entire game.

The catch? They're genuinely rare. With a base drop rate of roughly 1 in 350 while fishing, you can't just stumble into a stockpile — you need a strategy. This guide breaks down every farming method, the math behind drop rates, and exactly how to spend your relics for maximum value.

## How to Get Fisch Relics: All Four Methods

There are four reliable ways to add Enchant Relics to your inventory. Some require luck, some require cash, and some only appear during limited-time events.

| Method | Cost / Requirement | Drop Rate or Price |
|---|---|---|
| Fishing | Any rod (luck helps) | 1/350 base |
| Treasure chests | Treasure map + 250C$ fix fee | 6.3% per chest |
| Merlin (Sunstone Island) | Level 30+ | 11,000C$ each |
| Event rewards | Limited events (FischFright, Fischmas) | Varies by event |

### Fishing for Relics

Fishing is the most common route, and location makes a massive difference. Most areas offer that 1/350 baseline, but The Depths bumps your odds to 1/100. When an Aurora Borealis event is active, your chances jump all the way to 1/33 — a better than 10x improvement over the base rate.

Helpful details to know before you cast:

- **Aurora Borealis spawns randomly**, but you can force one with an Aurora Totem, purchased for 500,000C$ at the Brine Pool. Expensive, but worth it if you're serious about relic farming.
- **High-luck rods improve your odds.** There's no single "relic rod," but luck is the stat that matters.
- **Watch for the tell:** when a relic is hooked, the exclamation point above your head turns light aqua and you'll hear a bell sound. Don't mistake it for a normal fish.

### Treasure Chests

Fishing up a treasure map (1/160 chance, improved by the Sunken Rod) starts a mini-quest. Take the map to Jack Marrow in Forsaken Shores, pay 250C$ to have it fixed, then follow the coordinates to dig up a chest. Each chest carries a 6.3% chance of containing an Enchant Relic — not amazing on its own, but a solid bonus when you're map hunting anyway.

### Buying from Merlin

Once you hit level 30, Merlin's hut on Sunstone Island (coordinates X: -943, Y: 224, Z: -988) sells relics outright at 11,000C$ apiece. If you have money to burn, this is the fastest, most deterministic method. You can also sell spare relics back for 3,500C$, though that's a significant loss — hang onto them instead.

### Event Rewards

FischFright 2024 handed out three relics for turning in four witch's ingredients to Beatrix, and Fischmas 2024 included them in Unique and Supreme Presents. Based on player experience, Fisch tends to repeat these reward structures during seasonal reruns, so keep an eye on official announcements on the [official Fisch game page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/16732694052/Fisch) when events roll around.

## Drop Rate Comparison: Where Should You Farm?

| Location / Condition | Relic Chance | Notes |

| The Depths | 1/100 (1%) | Best permanent farming spot |

The math is clear: an Aurora Borealis session in The Depths is the dream scenario, since community reports suggest event modifiers stack with location bonuses. If you can't force an Aurora, grinding The Depths with a high-luck rod is your best consistent option.

## How to Use Fisch Relics: Enchanting at the Keeper's Altar

The primary use for Fisch relics is enchanting. Here's the step-by-step process:

| Step | Action | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Travel to the Statue of Sovereignty | Free |
| 2 | Enter the Sovereignty Mines at **night** | Free |
| 3 | Ride the mining elevator with Cole | 400C$ |
| 4 | Equip the rod you want enchanted | — |
| 5 | Hold an Enchant Relic and interact with the Keeper's Altar | 1 relic |

Important things to understand before you spend relics:

- The enchantment applied is **random** out of 34 total options. Chasing one specific enchantment can easily eat several relics.
- All enchantments are beneficial, but some synergize poorly with certain rods — a luck enchantment on an already-maxed-luck rod is wasted potential.
- Keep spare relics handy until you land a roll you actually want.

## Relics as Currency: Rods That Require Enchant Relics

Three of Fisch's best rods are locked behind relic requirements, so budget accordingly:

| Rod | Relic Requirement | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Trident Rod | 5 standard Enchant Relics | Trident's Temple door |
| Rod of the Depths | 1 Abyssal + 1 Hexed Enchant Relic | Depths maze door |
| Rod of the Exalted One | Hexed, Crystallized, Greedy, Translucent, Atlantean, Fossilized, and Mosaic relics | Pedestals in Mushgrove Swamp |

The Rod of the Exalted One is the real relic sink — you'll need seven mutated variants, which means farming mutations with mutation-boosting rods or waiting for server events that raise mutation odds.

## Pro Tips for Faster Relic Farming

- **Prioritize the Scavenger enchantment.** Per community reports, Scavenger makes you 3x more likely to hook Enchant Relics, Nukes, Treasure Maps, and Threads. A Scavenger rod snowballs your relic income.
- **Best high-luck rods for relic hunting:** Fortune Rod, No-Life Rod, Seraphic Rod, Sunken Rod, Rod of the Exalted One, Rod of the Depths, Rod of the Forgotten Fang, and Rod of the Eternal King. Budget players can start with the Lucky Rod or Rapid Rod.
- **Ignore the Relic Rod's name.** Despite the branding, it has no bonus for catching relics — and it's no longer obtainable anyway. The name refers to its bone-built, ancient appearance.
- **Farm at night and plan around events.** The altar only works at night, and mutation or Aurora events are your windows for rare relic variants.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the odds of catching Fisch relics while fishing?

The base chance is 1 in 350 in most areas, improving to 1 in 100 in The Depths and 1 in 33 during an Aurora Borealis. High-luck rods and the Scavenger enchantment further improve those numbers.

### How much do Enchant Relics cost from Merlin?

Each relic costs 11,000C$, and you must be level 30 or higher. Merlin's hut sits on Sunstone Island at X: -943, Y: 224, Z: -988.

### Can Enchant Relics be mutated?

Yes. Relics mutate just like fish, and you can boost mutation odds with mutation-boosting rods or by fishing during mutation-increasing server events. Mutated relics are required for the Rod of the Exalted One.

### What's the fastest way to farm Fisch relics?

Save up 500,000C$ for an Aurora Totem if possible — forcing an Aurora Borealis gives you the best odds in the game. Otherwise, grind The Depths with your highest-luck rod, and fish treasure maps on the side with a Sunken Rod.
