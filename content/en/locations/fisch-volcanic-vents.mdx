## What Are the Volcanic Vents in Fisch?

Between rising body heat, instant-death lava, and valuable deep-sea catches, **Fisch Volcanic Vents** can punish an unprepared player within seconds. This location matters because it is the first layer of Mariana’s Veil and the gateway to deeper areas, equipment upgrades, and a specialized bestiary. Learning the **Fisch Volcanic Vents** route before entering can save you from repeated deaths and wasted fishing trips.

The zone is built around towering hydrothermal formations, lava-covered terrain, and a parkour route leading to its main fishing area. It also introduces an environmental heat mechanic: the longer you remain exposed, the more dangerous your body temperature becomes.

Here is a quick overview of what awaits:

## How to Reach Fisch Volcanic Vents

You cannot simply sail to this area with a standard boat. Reaching **Fisch Volcanic Vents** requires a constructed Submarine and a trip beneath Roslit Bay.

The general route is straightforward, but the transition from open water to a hazardous interior catches many first-time visitors off guard.

| Step | What to do | Preparation tip |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Travel to Roslit Bay | Bring your intended bait before beginning the dive |
| 2 | Construct or obtain access to the Submarine | Complete any required submarine-building objectives first |
| 3 | Enter the water near the coral reef | Look for the cave opening around the middle of the reef |
| 4 | Pilot the Submarine through the underwater cave | Move carefully instead of rushing through narrow passages |
| 5 | Enter the first layer of Mariana’s Veil | Be ready for the heat system to become relevant |
| 6 | Cross the lava parkour route | Watch the timing of erupting lava fountains |
| 7 | Reach the fishing and upgrade area | Identify safe platforms before equipping your rod |

### Preparing before the dive

Inventory planning is more important here than in ordinary fishing locations. Returning to the surface because you forgot Fish Head or Truffle Worm adds unnecessary travel time.

A practical loadout includes:

- **Fish Head** for the Blisterback Blenny
- **Truffle Worms** for the Scalding Swordfish and Magma Leviathan
- **Worms** for the Hellfire Haddock
- **Flakes** for the Embertail Eel
- **Insects** for the Infernal Iguanafish

- Heat-resistant equipment, if already available

You should also decide what you want from the trip. A progression run should prioritize upgrade resources, while a bestiary run may require waiting for specific seasons, weather, or time periods.

## Heat, Lava, and Survival Strategy

The central challenge in **Fisch Volcanic Vents** is not catching fish—it is staying alive long enough to do so. Heat builds while you remain inside the layer, and environmental mistakes can sharply accelerate the danger.

Lava is the most unforgiving hazard. According to community reports, touching the lava results in immediate death rather than ordinary chip damage. Lava fountains are different: passing through an active eruption can cause heavy health loss while also adding substantial heat.

| Hazard | Effect | Recommended response |
|---|---|---|
| Ambient heat | Temperature rises over time | Complete objectives efficiently and use heat protection |
| Lava floor | Instant death on contact | Jump deliberately and avoid cutting corners |
| Lava fountains | Heavy damage and additional heat | Wait for the eruption to stop before crossing |
| Long fishing sessions | Extends heat exposure | Monitor temperature and prioritize target catches |
| Poor visibility | Makes platform edges harder to judge | Slow down and position the camera before jumping |
| Rushed parkour | Increases the chance of falling | Use controlled jumps and land near platform centers |

### Which equipment protects against heat?

The primary progression reward is the **Glimmerfin Suit Level 1**, which prevents the zone’s heat from overwhelming the player. Community-sourced information also indicates that Scoria Armor can stop heat from accumulating.

The Winter Cloak, despite its cold-weather theme, reportedly does not provide the same protection inside this layer.

| Equipment | Effective in the vents? | Function |
|---|---:|---|
| Glimmerfin Suit Level 1 | Yes | Protects against dangerous overheating |
| Scoria Armor | Yes | Prevents or counters heat accumulation |
| Winter Cloak | No | Does not provide the necessary vent protection |
| No protective equipment | Risky | Forces the player to limit time in the area |

Player experience suggests that body temperature falls slowly while suitable protective gear is equipped and does not drop below approximately 37 degrees. Treat this value as community-reported behavior rather than a permanent rule, since environmental mechanics can be rebalanced.

### Parkour tips that reduce deaths

Use the following approach when crossing the lava section:

1. **Observe before moving.** Watch each lava fountain complete at least one eruption cycle.
2. **Center every landing.** Landing on a platform’s edge leaves little room to correct your position.
3. **Move one obstacle at a time.** Do not plan three jumps ahead while a nearby fountain is active.
4. **Avoid following another player blindly.** Network delay and different movement timing can make their route unsafe for you.
5. **Secure the destination platform first.** Do not open menus or switch equipment until your character is stable.

Although the fishing pool looks like lava, rods can reportedly be used there regardless of their durability characteristics. That exception does not make the surrounding lava safe to touch.

## Upgrade Quest and Volcanic Rod Location

The **Fisch Volcanic Vents** upgrade station is essential to Mariana’s Veil progression. Completing its resource objective awards two important items:

- **Glimmerfin Suit Level 1**, providing the protection needed to operate in the hot layer
- **Submarine Heat Upgrade**, allowing the Submarine to travel farther into the deep-water route

Once both upgrades are obtained, you can access an underwater door that leads deeper into the cave system.

Available community documentation contains a version-related discrepancy: it mentions several required resources and four placements, but only clearly identifies two catches. Rather than assuming the missing requirements, inspect every slot at the live upgrade station.

| Confirmed fish resource | Preferred conditions | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Inferno Hide | Any bait, time, weather, and season | Accessible quest resource that can be caught throughout the year |
| Blisterback Blenny | Fish Head; daytime; clear weather; summer | Rarer progression catch that benefits from luck-focused equipment |

Inferno Hide is categorized as a relatively accessible catch, although random chance can still require several attempts. The Blisterback Blenny is substantially rarer and should be targeted under its preferred conditions.

For an efficient upgrade run:

- Catch Inferno Hide whenever it appears rather than postponing it.
- Save Fish Head bait for a clear summer day.
- Equip your best luck-oriented rod for the Blisterback Blenny.
- Verify all upgrade-station slots before leaving the layer.
- Place collected resources into the station and confirm both rewards.
- Equip or activate the new upgrades before approaching the deeper door.

### Best conditions to target first

Summer offers the largest cluster of condition-specific fish. During a summer visit, rotate your target according to weather:

| Summer conditions | Best targets |
|---|---|
| Clear day | Scalding Swordfish and Blisterback Blenny |
| Foggy day | Magma Leviathan |
| Rainy night | Hellfire Haddock |
| Any conditions | Inferno Hide |

Spring is the next most productive season. At night, use Flakes for the Embertail Eel or Seaweed for the Smoldering Stingray. When rain begins, switch to Minnow for the Molten Moray.

Autumn has a narrower objective: use Shrimp at night for the Pyrite Pufferfish. The Infernal Iguanafish can be attempted in any season, but it prefers windy nights.

### Efficient bestiary strategy

Bring at least one bait for every available condition, then organize your fishing around the hardest restrictions. For example, a clear summer day should not be spent targeting Inferno Hide because that fish is available under any conditions. Use the limited window for the Blisterback Blenny instead.

A sensible priority order is:

1. Target the Magma Leviathan during a foggy summer day.
2. Pursue the Blisterback Blenny and Scalding Swordfish during clear summer days.
3. Catch the Hellfire Haddock during rainy summer nights.
4. Complete the spring-specific catches.
5. Hunt the autumn Pyrite Pufferfish.
6. Fill unrestricted catches between weather changes.

Community reports describe the zone as a historically strong money-making location because of its concentration of high-rarity fish. However, sale values and catch rates may change between updates, so evaluate your earnings over a fixed sample—such as 20 catches—before committing to a long farming session.

## Fisch Volcanic Vents FAQ

### How do I enter Fisch Volcanic Vents?

Construct the Submarine at Roslit Bay, locate the underwater cave near the middle of the coral reef, and pilot the vessel through it. The Volcanic Vents are the first layer of Mariana’s Veil.

### How do I survive the heat in the Volcanic Vents?

Obtain and equip the Glimmerfin Suit Level 1 through the local upgrade objective. Community reports indicate that Scoria Armor also works, while the Winter Cloak does not. Avoid active lava fountains because they can increase heat while dealing heavy damage.

### What happens if I touch the lava?

Lava contact causes immediate death according to player experience. The lava fountains generally inflict severe damage and heat instead, but either hazard can end a run quickly. Wait for each eruption cycle and aim for the center of safe platforms.

### What is the rarest target in Fisch Volcanic Vents?

The Magma Leviathan is the area’s standout high-tier catch and prefers Truffle Worm bait during foggy summer days. The Blisterback Blenny is another important rare catch because it is used in the upgrade objective; target it with Fish Head during a clear summer day.
