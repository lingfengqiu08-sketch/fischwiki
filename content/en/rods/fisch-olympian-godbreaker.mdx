## What Is the Olympian Godbreaker in Fisch?

Few rods in Fisch carry as much mythic weight as the **Olympian Godbreaker**. Described in-game as "a forbidden construct forged from shattered divinity," this end-game rod has quickly become the community's favorite tool for farming C$, thanks to staggering luck, lure speed, control stats, and mutation-boosting passives. If you're chasing maximum profit per hour, the Olympian Godbreaker is currently considered the best C$-grinding rod in the game.

But raw power isn't the whole story. This rod has very specific unlock requirements, a handful of quirky drawbacks against certain event fish, and a mastery system that rewards dedicated grinding. In this guide, we'll break down everything: how to get it, how all five passives work, the best enchantment setups, and whether the grind is actually worth your time.

## How to Unlock the Olympian Godbreaker

The Olympian Godbreaker isn't something you stumble across. It's gated behind a serious checklist that will test your bestiary completion before you can even look at the price tag.

**Purchase requirements:**

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Level | 981+ |
| Cost | 50,000,000 C$ |
| Location | Olympian Fissure vendor |
| Prerequisite catch | Olympian Devil |
| Bestiary | 100% completion in five zones (see below) |

You'll need full bestiary completion in all of the following areas before the vendor will sell you the rod:

- Bellona's Frenzy of War
- Apollo's Song of Light
- Zeus' Thunder of Chaos
- Hades' Underworld of Indefinite
- Olympian Fissure

That's a hefty grind — five separate 100% bestiaries plus a 50 million C$ price tag. According to player experience, most anglers reach this point after several dozen hours of end-game play, so treat the Godbreaker as a long-term goal rather than an impulse purchase.

## Olympian Godbreaker Stats and All Five Passives

This rod's real strength lies in its passive suite. Here's a full breakdown:

| Passive | Effect | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st Passive | +35% Progress Speed; 30% chance for Cragged (7×); 30% chance for Gravitas (9.81×) | Massive mutation odds drive profit |
| 2nd Passive | 50% chance every 1.25s to pull the fish toward the bar (+3% progress if fish is on the bar) | Smooths out the reeling minigame |
| 3rd Passive | 30% chance per hook to trigger Starcaller Cry | Summons a black hole granting 2–5 bonus fish of the same or lower rarity |
| 4th Passive | 5% base Meteor Shower chance per cast; 12 meteors, +6% progress each (+72% total) | Chance grows +5% per non-triggering cast, resets on activation |
| 5th Passive | For locked fish with negative progress speed: sets speed to -75%, stronger bar-pull (+9%/+3% progress) | Meteor Shower cannot trigger while active |

The Starcaller Cry deserves a closer look. Beyond the bonus fish, it applies a 1.918× weight multiplier if the caught fish has Gravitas — a huge boost for value. The trade-off is a -0.15 control penalty and +5 disturbance for your next three catches, so timing matters. If you fail a perfect catch, the black hole is cancelled entirely.

Together, these passives explain why the Olympian Godbreaker dominates C$ farming: between Cragged's 7× and Gravitas' 9.81× multipliers, plus extra bonus fish from the black hole, a single good session can snowball into serious income.

## Where the Godbreaker Struggles

No rod is perfect, and the Olympian Godbreaker has one well-known weakness: Admin Event fish. Because the rod treats all progress-locked fish the same way, it performs poorly against fish with high Locked Progress Speed — the community frequently cites the Mango and its +400% Locked Progress Speed as a prime example.

| Scenario | Godbreaker Performance |
| --- | --- |
| Standard C$ grinding | Excellent — best in class |
| Mutation farming (Cragged/Gravitas) | Excellent |
| General fishing / rare hunts | Very good |
| Admin Event fish with locked progress speed | Below average |

If your sessions revolve around Admin Events, keep a specialized rod on hand and swap accordingly. For everything else, the Godbreaker is hard to beat.

## Mastery Quests and Rewards

The rod also has its own mastery track, rewarding dedicated use with meaningful bonuses:

| Quest | Task | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GRAND REWARD | Complete all other quests | Olympian Ascension skin |
| War Against Bellona | Perfect Catch 197 fish during a War Surge | +2% Olympian chance; +3% progress from the pulling passive |
| Apollo's Appeal | Catch 196 fish in light beams | +2% Olympian chance; Starcaller Cry control penalty reduced from -0.15 to -0.0981 |

Both mastery quests soften the rod's rough edges — the reduced control penalty from Apollo's Appeal is especially valuable since Starcaller Cry fires on 30% of hooks. Completing the full track earns the exclusive Olympian Ascension skin, a flashy cosmetic for end-game collectors. Community reports suggest knocking out both quests during boosted events or while farming the same zones required for bestiary completion to save time.

## Is the Olympian Godbreaker Worth 50 Million C$?

For most end-game players, yes — with caveats. The rod's combination of high lure speed, luck, and control, stacked with five powerful passives, makes it the strongest C$-grinding option currently available. The 30% odds on both Cragged and Gravitas mutations mean nearly every catch has a chance to multiply in value, and Starcaller Cry's bonus fish compound your earnings further.

However, the entry barrier is steep. Between the level 981 requirement, the Olympian Devil catch, five completed bestiaries, and the 50M C$ price, expect a significant time investment. If you're still mid-game, prioritize bestiary completion in the Olympian zones first — that progress counts toward the unlock anyway.

## FAQ

**How much does the Olympian Godbreaker cost in Fisch?**
It costs 50,000,000 C$ and is purchased from the vendor at Olympian Fissure after reaching level 981 and catching the Olympian Devil.

**Is the Olympian Godbreaker the best rod for C$ grinding?**
Based on current player experience, yes. Its mutation chances (Cragged and Gravitas), weight multipliers, and bonus-fish passive make it the top C$-farming rod, though it underperforms against certain Admin Event fish.

**Can the Olympian Godbreaker fail its black hole bonus?**
Yes. If you miss the perfect catch during Starcaller Cry, the black hole is cancelled and you lose the 2–5 bonus fish. Practice your perfect catches to maximize value.

**What's the best enchantment for the Fisch Olympian Godbreaker?**
Starforged Spirit paired with Sanctified is the go-to for profit, while Sea Prince as a secondary is popular with players who want easier handling during the reeling minigame.
