## Why Your Rod Path Matters in Fisch

If you're grinding Roblox's smash-hit fishing sim, a solid **Fisch progression guide** is the difference between pulling in 40C$ minnows and raking in six-figure payouts from the Deep Ocean. This **Fisch progression guide** breaks down the entire rod journey — from your rusty starter gear to the Trident Rod, the Rod of the Depths, and endgame legends like the Wingripper — without spending a single Robux.

The core loop is simple: upgrade your rod, catch heavier and rarer fish, earn more cash, and reinvest everything into the next upgrade. Each time you empty your wallet on a better rod, your earning rate jumps — so early upgrades pay for themselves fast.

## Early Game: From the Flimsy Rod to Your First Real Upgrade

You'll start with the Flimsy Rod, which can barely handle 10kg and has virtually no stats. Don't waste time on the Training Rod — community consensus is that you'll outgrow it within the hour. Here's the early-game checklist:

| Step | Rod / Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|-----------|------|-------|
| 1 | Flimsy Rod | Free | Catch fish around Moosewood to build your first 2,000C$ |
| 2 | Carbon Rod | 2,000C$ | Sold in Moosewood village; grab Angler quests across from the shop |
| 3 | Rowboat | Cheap | Buy from the Shipwright, then row to Roslit Island |
| 4 | Fungal Rod | Quest reward | Complete Agaric's Alligator task in Mushgrove Swamp |
| 5 | Steady Rod | 7,000C$ | Blacksmith on Roslit Island; handles sharks and the Colossal Squid |

The Fungal Rod deserves special mention. Per its in-game passive, every cast has roughly a 60% chance to grant a Luck IV-style boost for 45 seconds — a free buff you'll keep using well into the mid game, especially for bestiary grinding.

## Mid Game: Enchants, the Crystalized Rod, and the Mythical Rod

Once you're earning steady cash, the mid-game phase is where your income really starts to snowball.

**Enchant your rod early.** Take any Enchant Relics you've fished up to the Statue of Sovereignty and apply Hasty to offset poor Lure Speed, or a Luck enchant like Storming if you don't mind using the Shake mechanic. If you run out of relics, Merlin sells them for 11,000C$ each.

**Key mid-game rods:**

| Rod | Cost / Source | Why It's Good |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| Crystalized Rod | 35,000C$ + Oxygen Tank (Northern Expedition) | 1.75x Crystalized mutation multiplier; great all-round stats |
| Mythical Rod | ~110,000C$ via Traveling Merchant | 30% chance for a 4.5x Mythical mutation on fish value |
| Sunken Rod | Treasure Chests | 25% Treasure Map chance per 10 catches; Sunken mutation sells at 8.25x |
| Arctic Rod | 25,000C$ at Northern Summit | Applies a 1.3x Frozen mutation; reliable early money-maker |

The Crystalized Rod route is straightforward: climb the Northern Expedition mountain, fish up a Glass Diamond, and get another player to help thaw the rod. Use it near the base of Ancient Isle until you've banked enough for the Mythical Rod.

**Pro tip:** The Forsaken Shores pond is one of the best XP spots in the game thanks to the common Captain's Goldfish. Enchanting a rod with Clever or Wisdom speeds up level grinding even more.

## Late Game: Unlocking the Trident Rod

The Trident Rod is the gateway to serious money. It packs 150% Luck, periodically "stabs" rare fish to speed up catches, and rolls the Atlantean mutation for a 3x value multiplier. Here's what it takes:

| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Cash | 150,000C$ |
| Bestiary | Complete the Desolate Deep Bestiary |
| Relics | 5 Enchant Relics for the pedestals |
| Access | Diving Gear, then swim through the cave past the underwater fish corpse |

The Desolate Deep Bestiary is widely considered the hardest in the game, with player reports of multi-day grinds for the rarest entries. The full list includes Stalactite, Horseshoe Crab, Slate Tuna, Coral Geode, Phantom Ray, Cockatoo Squid, Rockstar Hermit Crab, Banditfish, Midnight Axolotl, Barbed Shark, Emperor Jellyfish, and the notoriously rare Sea Mine (use Crab Cages during a Fungal Rod luck boost).

Once done, place your five relics on the pedestals and buy the Trident Rod from the altar. Enchant it with Hasty for speed or Abyssal/Sea King for cash.

## Opening the Depths and Earning Big

With the Trident Rod equipped, your next goal is the Depths — and the 750,000C$ Rod of the Depths waiting inside. Follow these steps:

1. **Catch the Isonade.** Buy around 10 Crab Cages, find a Strange Whirlpool, and fish the surface. Whirlpools sit inside Isonade abundance events — use Truffle Worm bait and the Fungal Rod buff to speed things up.
2. **Complete the Vertigo Bestiary.** Dive into the whirlpool to reach Vertigo, then fish up Spiderfish, Twilight Eel, Fangborn Gar, Abyssacuda, Voidfin Mahi, Rubber Ducky, the Depths Key, and Night Shrimp (Crab Cages required).
3. **Unlock the door.** Speak to the ghost boy by the Depths Door with the Depths Key in hand (talk to him twice if needed).
4. **Farm beyond the door.** Based on community experience, fishing past the door during luck events averages roughly 50,000C$ every 10 minutes.

For the Rod of the Depths itself, you'll also need an Abyssal Relic and a Hexed Relic, plus the Advanced Diving Gear for the maze. Appraise relics during a Mutation multiplier event (or with an Abyssal-enchanted rod) to keep costs down. Once acquired, enchant it with Abyssal or Wormhole — don't cheap out at this stage.

| Depths Rod Stats | Value |
|-----------------|-------|
| Price | 750,000C$ |
| Lure Speed | 65% |
| Luck | 130% |
| Max Kg | 30,000kg |
| Passive | Free bonus fish every 3 catches |

## Endgame Goals: Kraken Rod, Heaven's Rod, and the Wingripper

Once you're past the Depths, your targets shift to rod crafting at Ancient Isle and the priciest rods in the game.

- **Kraken Rod (1,333,333C$ from Atlantis):** 185% Luck, 10% chance to double catches, 5% chance for a 10x Tentacle Surge mutation, plus a guaranteed rare fish every 50 catches.
- **Heaven's Rod (~2,000,000C$ total):** Requires a mountain expedition but offers a 5x mutation multiplier and higher base Luck than the Rod of the Depths.
- **Wingripper (Underground Music Venue questline):** The current endgame king — 222% Lure Speed and Luck, up to +222% progress speed at night, and mutations worth up to 16.2x.
- **Chrysalis Rod (same questline):** 90% chance for a 5x Bloom mutation and 100% Resilience with infinite Max Kg.

To unlock crafting at the Ancient Archive, you'll need three other players to open the door — but like the Depths Door, it stays open forever once unlocked, so do this early. Note that some crafted rods require level 500 (the No Life Rod benchmark), making full rod collection the game's toughest challenge.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best rod progression order in Fisch?**
The most efficient path runs: Flimsy → Carbon Rod (2,000C$) → Fungal Rod (quest) → Steady Rod (7,000C$) → Crystalized Rod (35,000C$) → Mythical Rod (~110,000C$) → Trident Rod (150,000C$) → Rod of the Depths (750,000C$) → Kraken or Heaven's Rod → Wingripper.

**How much money do you need to finish Fisch progression?**
Expect to spend well over 3,000,000C$ total across all major rods, though you'll earn each tier along the way. Fishing past the Depths Door during events is the fastest income source.

**What's the fastest way to make money in Fisch?**
Use your best rod with high-multiplier mutations (Mythical, Atlantean, Crystalized) during Mutation Surge events in the Depths or Deep Ocean, buffed by the Fungal Rod's luck boost.

**Is this Fisch progression guide free-to-play friendly?**
Yes — this Fisch progression guide requires no Robux, trades, or alts, only smart grinding, rod upgrades, and event timing.
