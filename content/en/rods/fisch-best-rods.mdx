## Quick Answer: What Are the Fisch Best Rods?

The single best rod in Fisch right now is the **Ethereal Prism Rod**, introduced in the Mariana's Veil update. It costs a staggering 15,000,000C$ and is found in the Calm Zone, but its 50% chance to apply an 8x value mutation — combined with 195% luck, near-instant lure speed, and infinite weight limit — means it pays for itself quickly.

## Starter Rods: Your First Real Upgrade

Every player begins with the Flimsy Rod, and its stats are exactly what they sound like. Skip the Training Rod entirely — its -70% luck penalty makes grinding painful. According to widespread community experience, one early purchase stands above everything else at this stage.

| Rod | Location | Price | Luck | Max Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flimsy Rod | Default inventory | Free | 0% | 10.4kg | Replace ASAP |
| Training Rod | Moosewood Merchant | 300C$ | -70% | 9kg | Avoid |
| Plastic Rod | Moosewood Merchant | 900C$ | 15% | 100kg | Budget option |
| **Carbon Rod** | Moosewood Merchant | 2,000C$ | 25% | 600kg | **Best starter** |
| Lucky Rod | Moosewood Merchant | 5,250C$ | 60% | 175kg | Tempting but flawed |

The **Carbon Rod** is the consensus best early rod among Fisch veterans. Its balanced stats, 25% luck boost, and 600kg weight cap make it a genuine workhorse for just 2,000C$. You might be eyeing the Lucky Rod's 60% luck, but its slow lure speed, poor resilience, and light weight limit will make every grind session feel like a slog. Resist the temptation.

**Pro tip:** Farm an Abundance Pool to earn money fast — these zones boost your odds of catching specific (often valuable) fish, making that 2,000C$ goal achievable in a single session.

## Tier 2 and Tier 3: The Steady Rod Changes Everything

Once you've outgrown the Carbon Rod, the **Steady Rod** from the Roslit Bay Blacksmith becomes the best mid-game investment in the game. For 7,000C$, you get a 100,000kg weight limit, 30% resilience, and a bigger Shake button — perfect for hauling in high-value catches in open water.

Its glaring weakness is a brutal -60% lure speed. The fix? Enchant it with **Hasty** and equip good bait, and it can carry you deep into tier 4 content.

Tier 3 is considered optional by many experienced players, but if you're heading to the Northern Expedition, these picks shine:

| Rod | Price | Luck | Max Weight | Standout Feature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arctic Rod | 25,000C$ | 65% | 80,000kg | Great for solo players |
| Avalanche Rod | 35,000C$ | 68% | 65,000kg | Strong all-rounder |
| Crystalized Rod | 35,000C$ | 45% | 25,000kg | 12% chance of 1.75x mutation |
| Boreal Rod | 42,000C$ | 50% | 14,000kg | 30% chance of 4x mutation |

The Crystalized Rod's mutation passive makes it the better long-term money-maker, though it requires a puzzle (easier with a friend's help). Solo players should default to the Arctic Rod.

## Tier 4 to Tier 6: Where the Money Starts Flowing

The **Mythical Rod**, sold by the Travelling Merchant for 110,000C$, dominates tier 4 with a 30% chance of a 4.5x value mutation. Pair it with the Hasty enchant and Rapid Catchers bait for maximum earning speed. Its low weight limit is the only real drawback.

At tier 5, the classic **Trident Rod** (150,000C$, Desolate Deep) offers 150% luck, a fish-stunning "stab" effect, and a 30% chance of 3x mutations — but completing the required bestiary is a grind. The Volcanic Rod from Mariana's Veil is a strong alternative with infinite weight and a 5x mutation passive, at double the price.

Tier 6 is where things get serious:

| Rod | Price | Key Passive | Best Enchant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rod of the Depths | 750,000C$ | Ghost catches a bonus fish every 3rd catch | Hasty |
| Auric Rod | Free (Sunken Chests) | Every catch worth 2–5x | Divine |
| Rod of the Exalted One | Free | 2.5x Exalted Relic chance | — |

The Rod of the Depths' ghost passive effectively gives you a free second fish every third catch — an income machine once you're reeling in expensive fish. Meanwhile, the Auric Rod is a lottery-ticket pickup: if a Sunken Chests server event spawns one early, it can carry you for a long time.

## Endgame Rods: Heaven's, Kraken, and Ethereal Prism

At tier 7, the community remains split between two monsters:

- **Heaven's Rod** (1,750,000C$, Northern Summit): Chart-topping 225% luck, infinite weight, and a 15% chance of 5x value mutations. Obtainable solo, though total costs approach 2,000,000C$ once you factor in prerequisites.
- **Kraken Rod** (1,333,333C$, Kraken Pool in Atlantis): 10% chance to catch an identical fish, 5% chance of a 10x mutation, and a guaranteed Legendary+ fish every 50th catch.

Both pair best with the **Hasty enchant**. If you prioritize raw reliability, go Heaven's; if you love high-roll upside, the Kraken Rod is tough to beat.

For the true endgame, the Ethereal Prism Rod rules the First Sea. One critical enchanting note: avoid enchants that apply their own mutations (like Abyssal or Anomalous), as they can overwrite the rod's 8x mutation with weaker ones. Run **Sturdy** initially, then upgrade to an Exalted Enchant such as **Sea Overlord** as soon as possible.

## Second Sea Rods: The Great Rod of Oscar

The Second Sea update (March 29, 2025) added a soft-reboot progression system with its own E$ currency. The crown jewel here is the **Great Rod of Oscar** — 5,000,000E$, 280% luck, +30% progress speed, 1.25x XP, and a 5% chance of a 12x mutation. The Carrot Rod is a fun runner-up, with a passive that can grant nearby players 8x mutations, making it great for group play.

## FAQ: Fisch Best Rods Questions Answered

**What is the best rod in Fisch overall?**
The Ethereal Prism Rod from the Mariana's Veil update. Its 50% chance of an 8x value mutation, combined with elite base stats, makes it the top earner in the First Sea.

**What is the best rod for beginners in Fisch?**
The Carbon Rod, hands down. At 2,000C$, it offers balanced stats and a 600kg weight limit — ideal for grinding your way up the ladder.

**Which rod makes the most money in Fisch?**
Per community reports, the Ethereal Prism Rod generates the highest income per hour once paired with a Sea Overlord Exalted Enchant. On a budget, the Mythical Rod's 4.5x mutation passive is the best early money-maker.

**What is the best enchant for most rods in Fisch?**
Hasty. Most of the best rods (Steady, Mythical, Trident, Rod of the Depths, Heaven's) suffer from slow lure speed, and Hasty fixes that bottleneck — dramatically increasing your catches per hour.
