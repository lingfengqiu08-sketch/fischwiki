A single rare catch can transform your favorite fishing rod—or complete one of several important progression objectives. The **Fisch enchant relic** matters because it applies a random regular enchantment, potentially improving everything from lure speed to valuable mutation rates. This guide explains where to find a **Fisch enchant relic**, how its drop rates work, and when you should save one instead of immediately using it.

## What Is a Fisch Enchant Relic?

An Enchant Relic is a regionless Relic-class catch in Fisch. Its primary purpose is to apply a random regular enchantment to the fishing rod you currently have equipped at the Keepers Altar.

Unlike ordinary fish, the relic has no preferred bait, season, weather, or time. Its standard fishing chance is fixed rather than improved by the usual Luck stat, so stacking generic Luck bonuses is not an effective farming strategy.

Here are its core statistics:

| Attribute | Enchant Relic value |
|---|---:|
| Classification | Relic |
| Standard catch chance | 1 in 350 |
| Approximate catch rate | 0.29% per eligible catch |
| Region | Regionless |
| Preferred bait | None |
| Preferred weather | None |
| Preferred season | None |
| Preferred time | None |
| XP value | 800 XP |
| Base weight | 21 kg |
| Base sale value | 3,500 C$ |
| Main purpose | Applying a random rod enchantment |

Although selling a Fisch enchant relic provides 3,500 C$ at its base weight, that is usually not its best use. Relics are comparatively difficult to replace, while currency can be earned through repeatable fishing. Unless you already have a large reserve and no unfinished relic objectives, keeping it is generally more valuable.

Catching your first one also awards the **Divine Relic** badge.

## How to Get a Fisch Enchant Relic

The standard method is fishing in an eligible body of water. Under the currently listed mechanics, non-Depths fishing locations provide a fixed **1-in-350 chance**, or approximately **0.29%**, for each eligible catch.

Because the relic is regionless, you do not need to search for a particular island or fishing spot. Choose a location where you can complete catches rapidly and reliably. Faster catches create more opportunities per hour even when the probability per catch remains unchanged.

### Drop rates and improvements

Two specialized effects can improve the relic rate: the **Scavenger enchantment** and the **Relic Construct companion**. These modifiers are more useful than ordinary Luck because they specifically affect relic acquisition.

| Farming setup | Approximate chance | Percentage | Improvement over base rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| No relevant bonus | 1 in 350 | 0.29% | Base |
| Relic Construct companion | 1 in 175 | 0.57% | About 2× |
| Scavenger enchantment | 1 in 117 | 0.85% | About 3× |
| Scavenger + Relic Construct | 1 in 58.3 | 1.71% | About 6× |

Combining Scavenger with the Relic Construct is the strongest listed repeatable setup. At roughly 1 in 58.3, it does not guarantee a relic every 58 catches; it means that 58.3 catches is the long-run statistical average per success.

A Personal Aquarium can also produce an Enchant Relic at a listed **2.09% chance**. Because an aquarium reward roll is not the same as an ordinary fishing cast, avoid directly comparing 2.09% with the per-catch rates above.

Certain Admin Events may provide additional opportunities. Recent update history has included relic availability during events such as:

- Relic Storm
- Keepers Krypt
- Classic Commotion

Event rules can change between updates, so check the live event description before choosing a farming method. Fallen Stars are no longer a source as of version 1.83.0, making older guides that recommend them outdated.

You can verify that you are launching the authentic experience through the [official Fisch page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/16732694052/Fisch).

### What the odds mean in practice

Random drops are unpredictable over short sessions. One player may catch a relic immediately, while another may go hundreds of catches without seeing one. That difference is normal player experience and does not necessarily indicate that the system is broken.

The following estimates show the probability of finding at least one relic after a set number of eligible catches:

| Setup and catches | Estimated chance of at least one relic |
|---|---:|
| Base rate, 100 catches | 24.9% |
| Base rate, 350 catches | 63.3% |
| Base rate, 500 catches | 76.1% |
| Relic Construct, 100 catches | 43.6% |
| Scavenger, 100 catches | 57.6% |
| Scavenger + Relic Construct, 100 catches | 82.3% |

These figures are probability estimates calculated from the listed drop rates, not guarantees. Even at an 82% cumulative probability, approximately 18% of comparable sessions would still end without a relic.

## Best Fisch Enchant Relic Farming Strategy

The most efficient approach is to maximize eligible catches per hour while using every relic-specific modifier available to you. A location with slightly less valuable fish may outperform a difficult fishing zone if it lets you finish catches much faster.

Use this farming sequence:

| Step | Action | Why it helps |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Equip a rod with Scavenger | Improves the listed rate from about 0.29% to 0.85% |
| 2 | Activate the Relic Construct companion | Adds another relic-specific bonus |
| 3 | Fish in an eligible non-Depths body of water | Uses the standard fixed relic drop pool |
| 4 | Prioritize fast, consistent catches | More completed catches mean more drop rolls |
| 5 | Claim Personal Aquarium opportunities | Provides another potential acquisition route |
| 6 | Watch for relevant Admin Events | Temporary events may create extra opportunities |
| 7 | Store relics until you know your goal | Prevents accidentally spending progression materials |

Generic Luck is not the answer here. The normal 1-in-350 rate is described as fixed and unaffected by standard Luck, so a high-Luck rod without Scavenger should not be treated as a dedicated relic-farming rod.

Other practical ways to improve your results include:

- **Reduce failed catches.** A theoretically fast setup is not useful if difficult fish repeatedly escape.
- **Avoid unnecessary travel.** Since the relic is regionless, select a convenient fishing area.
- **Track completed catches, not minutes.** Drop probability is tied more directly to opportunities than session length.
- **Farm in longer batches.** Larger samples will not guarantee success, but they make your results more likely to approach the expected average.
- **Do not chase weather changes.** Weather, season, and time provide no listed preference for this catch.
- **Avoid special bait waste.** The relic has no preferred bait, so save expensive bait unless it benefits the other fish you are targeting.

Information surrounding The Depths requires care. Current data lists the standard fixed fishing method as excluding The Depths, while also noting that its catch pool can indirectly produce unusual relic behavior. For predictable farming, use an eligible non-Depths location unless a current event or patch specifically states otherwise.

## How to Use an Enchant Relic at the Keepers Altar

To spend the relic, travel to the **Keepers Altar beneath the Statue of Sovereignty**. The most important detail is that the effect applies to your **currently equipped rod**.

Before interacting with the altar, verify the rod in your hand. Using a relic while the wrong rod is equipped can waste a rare resource or replace an enchantment you wanted to keep.

A safe process looks like this:

1. Place the Fisch enchant relic in your inventory.
2. Travel to the Keepers Altar under the Statue of Sovereignty.
3. Equip the exact fishing rod you want to modify.
4. Review its existing enchantment before continuing.
5. Use the relic at the altar.
6. Inspect the newly rolled regular enchantment.
7. Decide whether to keep it or spend another relic on a reroll.

Each regular enchantment initially has an equal **1-in-29 chance**, or approximately **3.45%**, of appearing. When the rod already has a regular enchantment, the listed rate for each eligible outcome becomes **1 in 28**, or about **3.57%**.

That makes targeting one exact enchantment expensive. Assuming independent rolls and an unchanged eligible pool, your approximate chance of seeing a specific result is:

| Number of rolls | Chance of getting a specific 1-in-29 enchantment |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3.45% |
| 5 | 16.1% |
| 10 | 29.6% |
| 20 | 50.4% |
| 29 | 63.9% |
| 40 | 75.5% |

These probabilities explain why it is wise to build a relic reserve before chasing a perfect roll. Even 20 attempts leave close to a 50% chance of missing one particular enchantment.

### Example enchantment results

The complete regular enchantment pool contains many possible outcomes. Two useful examples are Abyssal and Blessed:

| Enchantment | Main effects | Suitable use |
|---|---|---|
| Abyssal | 20% Abyssal mutation chance at a 5.5× value modifier; weight outcomes include an 80% chance for +30% weight and a 20% chance for -40% weight | Rods without a strong built-in mutation rate |
| Blessed | Bonus chance for Shiny and Sparkling catches, plus 5% lure speed and 5% progress speed | Collecting rare visual variants while maintaining balanced fishing speed |

Abyssal offers greater value potential but includes a negative weight possibility. Blessed is a steadier option for players focused on Shiny and Sparkling collections.

Do not reroll automatically because an enchantment is not the exact one you planned to obtain. Compare the result with the rod’s natural strengths first. A useful secondary result can be better than consuming your entire relic stock while pursuing a single ideal outcome.

## Should You Enchant, Sell, or Save Your Relics?

Enchanting rods is only one use for these items. A Fisch enchant relic can also be required for unlocking locations, completing secrets, obtaining specialized equipment, or interacting with relic-related systems.

Known progression uses include:

| Objective | Why a relic may be needed |
|---|---|
| Trident’s Temple | Door-opening requirement |
| The Depths Maze | Progression component |
| Underground Music Venue | Access requirement |
| Cultist Lair | Access or progression component |
| Thalassar’s Secret | Secret progression |
| The Keeper’s Secret | Used while pursuing the Rod of the Exalted One |
| Relic Construct | Can be fed to the construct |

Your best choice depends on your account stage:

- **Newer players:** Save the first few relics for progression requirements.
- **Midgame players:** Reserve enough for known doors and secrets before rerolling rods.
- **Late-game players:** Use excess relics to optimize specialized rods.
- **Collectors:** Keep a buffer for future updates, events, or newly added relic interactions.
- **Currency-focused players:** Selling is possible, but 3,500 C$ is generally modest compared with the relic’s utility.

A sensible rule is to keep a protected reserve that you never use for random rolls. Once your major relic-gated objectives are complete, place extra relics into a separate enchanting budget. This approach prevents a streak of unsuccessful rerolls from blocking later progression.

## FAQ

### What is the base Fisch enchant relic drop rate?

The standard rate is **1 in 350**, or approximately **0.29% per eligible catch**. Scavenger improves it to roughly 1 in 117, the Relic Construct improves it to 1 in 175, and combining both produces an approximate 1-in-58.3 rate.

### Does Luck increase the Enchant Relic catch chance?

Ordinary Luck does not affect its fixed standard catch rate. Use relic-specific effects such as the Scavenger enchantment and Relic Construct companion instead.

### Where do I use an Enchant Relic?

Use it at the Keepers Altar beneath the Statue of Sovereignty. Equip the intended rod before interacting because the relic enchants the rod you currently have equipped.

### Should I sell my first Enchant Relic?

Usually not. Its 3,500 C$ base value is less important than its ability to enchant rods and satisfy multiple progression requirements. Saving your first several relics is typically the safer choice.
