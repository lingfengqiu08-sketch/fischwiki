## Fishing Efficiently Once You Arrive

After locating the Fisch Obsidian Trench, resist the urge to cast randomly. A controlled test makes it much easier to identify which variables are actually improving your results.

Start by recording four conditions:

- In-game time
- Current weather
- Bait equipped
- Rod equipped

Make five to ten casts without changing anything. Then adjust only one variable. For example, switch bait while keeping the same rod, time window, and fishing position.

If you change your bait, rod, enchantment, weather, and position simultaneously, you will not know which change produced the better catch.

### Use a Simple Session Log

| Attempt block | Rod | Bait | Conditions | Casts | Notable result |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Baseline | Balanced rod | General bait | Current conditions | 5 | Record normal catches |
| Bait test | Same rod | Alternative bait | Same conditions | 5 | Compare rarity and bite rate |
| Rod test | Luck-focused rod | Best-performing bait | Same conditions | 5 | Compare encounter quality |
| Control test | High-control rod | Same bait | Same conditions | 5 | Compare successful catches |
| Final block | Best combination | Best bait | Preferred conditions | 10 | Focus on target farming |

This 30-cast example gives you enough observations to spot obvious differences without pretending to deliver scientifically exact drop rates. Random number generation can still create streaks, so avoid declaring a bait “best” after only one catch.

### Confirm the Fishing Boundary

One of the most important location tips is to test more than one casting position. Two players standing a short distance apart may be fishing in different regional pools if one cast crosses an invisible boundary.

Try these positions:

- Near the center of the suspected zone
- Against each major wall or ledge
- Beside unique structures
- At different water elevations
- On both sides of an entrance

Watch for changes in catch names, bestiary progress, ambient effects, music, or area text. Those indicators are more useful than simply copying where another avatar stands.
