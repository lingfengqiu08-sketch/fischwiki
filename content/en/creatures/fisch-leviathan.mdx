The Leviathan belongs to the Exotic rarity tier and is associated primarily with the Cultist Lair. It awards a substantial 20,000 XP when caught, while its natural weight can range from 200,000 kg to 798,000 kg.

Its size is only part of the challenge. The fish has a locked **-95% progress-speed modifier**, meaning that most ordinary modifiers cannot remove or meaningfully alter that penalty. It also has Passive Block Level 2, reducing the chance of obtaining it through passive effects.

## How to Beat the Leviathan’s Whip Attack

The reeling minigame is what makes the Fisch Leviathan especially dangerous. Once your reeling progress exceeds 50%, the fish can use a special whip attack.

While you remain above that threshold, the game checks for the attack every four seconds. The initial activation chance is 15%. After each attack, that chance drops by 1.5 percentage points until reaching a minimum of 7.5%.

A whip has two simultaneous effects:

- It removes **35% of your current reeling progress**
- It reduces your Control bar by **60% for three seconds**

Version 1.57.2 limited the whip to a maximum of three activations during a catch.

### Understanding the progress loss

The whip removes 35% of your progress at that moment; it does not simply subtract a fixed 35 percentage points in every situation. That distinction changes how much progress remains.

| Progress before whip | Progress lost | Approximate progress remaining |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 55% | 19.25 points | 35.75% |
| 60% | 21 points | 39% |
| 75% | 26.25 points | 48.75% |
| 80% | 28 points | 52% |
| 90% | 31.5 points | 58.5% |
| 100% | 35 points | 65% |

For example, a whip at 80% progress removes 28 percentage points because 35% of 80 is 28. Your progress would fall to approximately 52%, not 45%.

### Practical reeling strategy

The locked -95% progress speed makes patience more valuable than aggressive corrections. Use measured movements and anticipate the temporary loss of control once you cross 50%.

1. **Treat 50% as the danger line.** The whip checks begin only after you move above it.
2. **Expect a check every four seconds.** Do not assume that a quiet period means the attack can no longer happen.
3. **Center your positioning when possible.** A centered fish marker gives you more room to react when the bar shrinks.
4. **Avoid overcorrecting during the three-second debuff.** Large inputs can make a reduced Control bar harder to manage.
5. **Expect more than one attack.** The Leviathan can whip up to three times.
6. **Stay calm after losing progress.** A whip can push you below 50%, temporarily removing the condition needed for another attack.
7. **Rebuild deliberately.** Returning above 50% starts exposing you to additional four-second checks again.

The first attack begins with a 15% activation chance per eligible check. If another check follows an attack, the chance is reduced by 1.5 percentage points. The general sequence therefore becomes less likely over repeated activations, although the fight remains dangerous because the Control bar penalty can cause a failed catch before progress is rebuilt.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do you catch the Fisch Leviathan?

The Fisch Leviathan is normally found in the Cultist Lair. During supported Admin Events such as Leviathan Rampage or Dragons!, it can also be caught at a fixed chance from any body of water.

### What makes the Leviathan difficult to catch?

It has a locked -95% progress-speed modifier and a whip attack that becomes possible above 50% reeling progress. The attack removes 35% of current progress and shrinks the Control bar by 60% for three seconds. It can activate up to three times in one attempt.

### How much is the Fisch Leviathan worth?

A minimum natural specimen is worth approximately 29,605.3 C$. Its true average sale value is about 42,927.6 C$, while a maximum natural specimen can sell for approximately 118,125 C$. External boosts or special variants may produce different results.

### Does luck help with catching Leviathan?

Luck favors the Leviathan’s normal Exotic encounter chance, but its final calculated catch chance is still subject to a divisor of five. Luck also does not cancel the locked reeling penalty or protect you from the whip mechanic. During fixed-chance Admin Events, follow the current event rules because the precise interaction with luck is not specified.
