## Efficient Farming Tips and Common Mistakes

The most efficient approach is to separate preparation from active fishing. Arrive in Astral Cavern, organize your gear, and identify the correct water before rain begins. Once the storm starts, spend the weather window casting rather than changing equipment or navigating.

### Recommended preparation checklist

- Reach Boreal Pines and enter Astral Cavern.
- Confirm that you can cast into the appropriate water.
- Equip a rod that does not add unnecessary luck.
- Remove optional luck bonuses when practical.
- Keep enough inventory space for repeated catches.
- Decide whether you are targeting the bestiary, XP, weight, or C$.
- Track catches if comparing loadouts.
- Take advantage of rain as soon as it begins.

### Mistakes that reduce efficiency

**Fishing outside Astral Cavern:** Boreal Pines is the region, but the named source is the cavern. Nearby water should not automatically be treated as equivalent.

**Using maximum luck without checking traits:** More luck is normally attractive, but it directly conflicts with this fish’s Unfavoring classification.

**Treating unconfirmed preferences as requirements:** The source data does not establish a required bait, season, or time. Advice claiming otherwise may reflect player experience, outdated data, or confusion with another species.

**Judging a setup after only a few casts:** Rare catches are affected by random variation. A short unsuccessful streak does not prove that your setup is invalid.

**Expecting every catch to sell for 882 C$:** That value applies to the maximum natural weight of 18.9 kg. The listed average is much lower at 256.7 C$.

**Ignoring the weather window:** Rain is the clearest environmental clue. If your goal is specifically this fish, prioritize rainy periods over routine cavern fishing in other conditions.

If the pike does not appear immediately, stay methodical. Verify location, confirm rain, lower avoidable luck, and continue collecting a meaningful sample rather than cycling through unsupported bait combinations.
