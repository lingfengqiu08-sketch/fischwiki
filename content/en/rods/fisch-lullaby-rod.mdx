The **Fisch Lullaby rod** is one of the most exciting rewards added in the Venue Takeover update, and for good reason. This Stage 12 rod combines a stunning celestial guitar design with mutation odds that can multiply your catch value by up to 6.5×. But the Fisch Lullaby rod doesn't come cheap in effort — you'll need to find a hidden island, survive a maze, and grind through a massive 12-part questline.

In this guide, we'll break down everything you need to know: where to find the rod, its full stat sheet, how its passive abilities work, and a step-by-step walkthrough of every quest Simon throws at you.

## What Is the Lullaby Rod in Fisch?

The Lullaby rod debuted with the Venue Takeover update on May 2, 2026, which introduced new rods, NPCs, and quests centered around the Underground Music Venue. Visually, it's a showstopper — a glowing celestial guitar wrapped in radiant white outlines, with rainbow energy rings orbiting its body and colorful wave-like trails flowing off the back.

It's classified as a **limited** rod, meaning it may not be obtainable forever. If you're serious about adding it to your collection, it's worth starting Simon's questline sooner rather than later.

## Lullaby Rod Passive Abilities Explained

The Lullaby rod's real power lies in its mutation chances and special mechanics. According to player experience, these passives make it one of the best value-multiplier rods in the game.

### Mutation Chances

| Mutation | Chance | Value Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| Prismatic | 24% | 6.5× |
| Rainbow | 18% | 6× |
| Mythical | 12% | 5.5× |
| Shiny or Sparkling | +2% | 1.85× |

Add it up, and over half of your catches will land a high-value mutation. That's an extraordinary rate compared to most rods in the game.

### Conditional and Special Effects

- **Nighttime bonus**: During Night, you get an extra +6% Prismatic chance — pushing that top-tier mutation to 30%. Night fishing is strongly recommended.
- **Moonlight Ray**: Every 2–4 measures, a Moonlight Ray blasts the fish you're reeling in. This flings the fish to the center of your bar, stuns it for 0.5 seconds, and grants +5% Progress. It's a built-in assist that makes long fights noticeably easier.

### Best Enchantments

Community reports consistently point to two enchantment setups:

| Setup | Enchantment | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| Weight hunting | Magnitude Might | Boosts Fish Weight, Control, and Resilience — patches the rod's weak Control stat |
| Grinding | Herculean + Cryogenic | Improves Control and Progress Speed for faster farm sessions |

The Lullaby rod's 0.25 Control is its main weakness, so an enchantment that shores that up is almost mandatory for endgame content.

## How to Get the Lullaby Rod: Finding Simon

The rod is locked behind a questline given by **Simon**, an NPC located on the Hawaii island hidden inside Mariana's Veil. Here's the fastest route, based on player experience:

1. Travel to **Mariana's Veil** and spawn your submarine to cross the cave system heading toward the Abyssal Zenith.
2. When you reach the **Angler Fish maze**, cross the first passage, then take a right into the second passage to enter a cave.
3. This drops you near Hawaii — swim to the island and you'll find Simon sitting on a chair.

Talk to Simon and the full 12-quest gauntlet begins. Fair warning: this is one of the longest questlines in Fisch, so prepare for a serious time investment.

### Tips for Surviving the Grind

- **Quest 5 is a material check.** Start stockpiling Sundial Totems and Iron Chunks early — 50 totems doesn't happen overnight.
- **Quest 6 costs 12,000,000 C$.** Keep saving throughout the questline so money never becomes the bottleneck.
- **Mutation-dependent quests (3, 9, 11, 12) are RNG-heavy.** Boost your Luck with gear and bait, and fish at night where possible to stack the odds in your favor.
- **Quest 8 requires the rod itself**, meaning you'll receive the Lullaby rod partway through the questline and finish "Encore" and beyond with it equipped. Practice the Metronome Beat minigame before attempting the six-fish streak.

If you're new to Fisch entirely, the official [Roblox Fisch game page](https://www.roblox.com/) is the best place to jump in and start leveling — the rod requires Level 500, so newcomers have a long road ahead.

## Is the Lullaby Rod Worth It?

For players at Level 500+, absolutely. The combination of infinite Max Kg, 225% Luck, and a 54%+ combined chance at Rainbow-tier or better mutations makes the Lullaby rod a top-tier money-making and collection tool. The Moonlight Ray mechanic even helps during fights, adding free progress every few measures.

The main drawbacks are the steep entry requirements: the Level 500 cap, the 12,000,000 C$ payment mid-questline, and one of the longest quest chains the game has seen. But if you're an endgame player hunting high-value mutations, the payoff justifies the grind.
