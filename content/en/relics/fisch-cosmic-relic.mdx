## What Is the Cosmic Relic in Fisch?

Few items in Fisch have changed the enchantment meta quite like the Fisch Cosmic Relic. Unlike traditional enchant stones, the Cosmic Relic grants an exclusive secondary set of enchantments—known as Cosmic Enchants—without removing or replacing your rod's regular enchantment. In other words, you get to stack two powerful buffs on a single rod, which is a massive deal for anyone grinding Scylla, Apex fish, or event rarities.

According to the in-game flavor text, it's "a mysterious shard pulsing with the rhythm of distant galaxies," capable of channeling forgotten forces drawn from the fabric of the cosmos itself. That sounds dramatic, but the real power is practical: more progress speed, more control, and more flexibility in how you build your rods.

The Cosmic Relic was introduced in Version 1.44.0 and has since become one of the most sought-after relics in the game. Below, we'll cover every obtainment method, the drop rates, the best ways to farm it, and how to decide between the nine possible Cosmic Enchants.

## Every Cosmic Relic Obtainment Method (With Drop Rates)

There are currently four ways to get your hands on a Cosmic Relic. As you can see from the table, they vary wildly in efficiency:

| Method | Drop Rate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fallen Stars (during Starfall) | **20%** | By far the most reliable method—open Fallen Stars while Starfall is active |
| Rod of the Cosmos (any water) | 0.1% | Increases to **2%** during Starfall |
| Rare Goods Dealer (lucky version) | 15% | The luckier shop variant heavily boosts your odds |
| Rare Goods Dealer (standard) | Unlisted | Lower odds than the lucky version |
| Personal Aquarium rewards | 0.05% | Passive income method—slow but zero effort |

### Which Method Should You Prioritize?

If you're actively hunting a Fisch Cosmic Relic, here's the optimal strategy, based on the numbers above:

- **During Starfall:** Fish with the Rod of the Cosmos (2% chance) and crack open every Fallen Star you find (20% chance per star). Combine both and you'll accumulate relics fast.
- **Outside of events:** Check the Rare Goods Dealer regularly. The lucky variant at 15% is your best bet until the next Starfall rolls around.
- **Idle players:** Keep your Personal Aquarium running. At 0.05%, it's a lottery ticket, but it costs you nothing to let rewards stack up.

Community reports consistently point to Fallen Stars during Starfall as the dominant farming method—the 20% rate simply dwarfs everything else in the game.

## How Cosmic Enchants Work

The core appeal of the Cosmic Relic is the dual-enchant system. When you apply a Cosmic Enchant, your rod keeps its regular enchantment while gaining a second, cosmic-tier effect in a separate slot. There are **nine possible Cosmic Enchants** you can roll, meaning outcomes are somewhat random—expect to burn through multiple relics if you're chasing a specific roll.

| Feature | Regular Enchant | Cosmic Enchant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slot | Primary | Secondary |
| Replaces existing enchant? | Yes | No |
| Source | standard enchant stones | Cosmic Relic |
| Roll pool | Standard enchant list | 9 cosmic-only enchants |

Because the cosmic enchant stacks on top of your regular one, you can create powerful combos. The most talked-about pairing in the community is **Overclocked + Piercing**, which accelerates reel progress while keeping difficult fish hooked.

## The 9 Cosmic Enchants: Which Ones Are Worth Rolling?

Only two Cosmic Enchants are fully documented so far, but they're both meta-relevant. Here's a breakdown:

| Enchant | Effect | Best Paired With |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overclocked** | +5% progress speed on every fish, including progress-locked fish like the Scylla | Piercing; best on Onirifalx or Polaris Serenade for faster Scylla and Apex catches |
| **Tenacity** | +20% progress speed per snapped reel, stacking infinitely; resets after a successful catch | Low-control rods when fishing for non-progress-locked fish |

### Overclocked: The Consistency Pick

Overclocked is the safe, universal choice. A flat 5% progress speed boost applies to *every* catch, and critically, it works on progress-locked fish—the dreaded Scylla and Apex-tier catches that normally have fixed progress bars. Pair it with Piercing on a top-tier rod like the Onirifalx or Polaris Serenade, and you'll shave meaningful time off every long fight.

### Tenacity: The High-Risk Gambler's Enchant

Tenacity rewards failure—in a good way. Every snapped reel grants +20% progress speed, and these stacks are infinite. If your rod has low control and you snap lines often, Tenacity can turn your weakness into a snowballing buff. The catch: the entire stack wipes once you land a fish, so it shines on sustained grinds rather than one-off trophy hunts.

## Farming Tips: Maximizing Your Cosmic Relic Haul

Want to build up a relic stockpile efficiently? Follow this community-tested checklist:

1. **Sync your grinding with Starfall events.** Both your Rod of the Cosmos catch rate (2%) and Fallen Star relic chance (20%) are boosted during this window.
2. **Always open Fallen Stars immediately.** Sitting on them delays your rolls, and each one is a 1-in-5 shot at a relic.
3. **Save relics until you can afford multiple rolls.** Since there are nine possible Cosmic Enchants, one relic rarely gives you what you want. Plan for 3–5 rolls minimum.
4. **Check the Rare Goods Dealer every session.** Stock rotates, and the lucky variant's 15% rate makes it the best off-event purchase.
5. **Don't sleep on Aquariums.** Even at 0.05%, Personal Aquarium rewards are free rolls over time.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even experienced players waste Cosmic Relics. Watch out for these pitfalls:

- **Rolling Tenacity on high-control rods.** If you rarely snap lines, the stacks never build. Save Tenacity for low-control setups.
- **Using Overclocked on fast-catch fish only.** The enchant's real value is on progress-locked fish—don't waste rolls if you're not chasing Scylla or Apex targets.
- **Ignoring Starfall windows.** A 20% rate versus 0.1% is a 200x difference. Grinding outside Starfall is statistically brutal.
- **Applying relics one at a time.** Rolling into the same unwanted enchant repeatedly is painful; batch your relics to smooth out RNG.
